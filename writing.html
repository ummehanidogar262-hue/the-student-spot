✍️ Writing

Learn how to write clearly, creatively and confidently.

📝 Welcome to Writing

Good writing helps you communicate your ideas clearly. Whether you are writing a paragraph, essay, letter, email, article, report, speech or story, organising your ideas is important.

💡 Student Tip: Before you start writing, understand the topic, organise your ideas, and decide what you want your reader to understand.

📝 Writing Basics

1. Understand the Topic

Read the question carefully and identify exactly what you have been asked to write.

2. Plan Your Ideas

Make a short plan before writing. Decide what information belongs in the introduction, main body and conclusion.

3. Use Clear Sentences

Keep your sentences understandable and make sure each sentence communicates a clear idea.

4. Check Your Work

Read your writing again and check spelling, grammar, punctuation and organisation.

📖 Paragraph Writing

A good paragraph usually focuses on one main idea. It can contain a topic sentence, supporting sentences and a concluding sentence.

Example structure:

Topic sentence → Supporting details → Example → Concluding sentence

Example Topic: My Favourite Hobby

Start by introducing the hobby. Explain why you enjoy it, give supporting details and finish by summarising your opinion.

📚 Essay Writing

An essay develops an idea or discusses a topic in organised paragraphs.

Introduction

Introduce the topic and give the reader an idea of what the essay will discuss.

Body Paragraphs

Explain your main points using reasons, examples and relevant details.

Conclusion

Summarise the main ideas without introducing a completely new topic.

Useful essay structure:

Introduction → Main Point 1 → Main Point 2 → Main Point 3 → Conclusion

✉️ Letter Writing

Formal Letters

Formal letters are used for official or serious communication. Use polite and respectful language.

Informal Letters

Informal letters are usually written to friends or family. The language can be more personal and relaxed.

Basic letter structure:

Address/Date → Greeting → Introduction → Main Message → Closing → Signature

📧 Email Writing

Emails should be clear, organised and appropriate for the person receiving them.

Subject

Use a short subject that clearly explains the purpose of the email.

Greeting

Start with an appropriate greeting.

Message

Explain the purpose of your email using clear paragraphs.

Closing

End politely with an appropriate closing.

📰 Article Writing

An article presents information or ideas for readers. It often has an interesting title and an engaging introduction.

Article structure:

Title → Introduction → Main Information → Examples → Conclusion

⭐ Tip

Choose a clear title and try to make the opening interesting enough to encourage the reader to continue.

📋 Report Writing

A report presents information in an organised and usually formal way.

Heading

Clearly state what the report is about.

Introduction

Explain the purpose or subject of the report.

Findings

Present the important information or observations.

Conclusion / Recommendations

Summarise the findings and provide recommendations when appropriate.

🎤 Speech Writing

A speech is written to be delivered to an audience. It should sound natural when spoken.

Speech structure:

Greeting → Introduction → Main Points → Examples → Conclusion → Thank You
