📖 Quick Fractions Guide

A fraction represents part of a whole. The top number is the numerator and the bottom number is the denominator.

Example:

3/5

3 = numerator
5 = denominator

Important rules

  • Simplify fractions whenever possible.
  • Use a common denominator when adding or subtracting.
  • To multiply fractions, multiply the numerators and denominators.
  • To divide fractions, multiply by the reciprocal.
