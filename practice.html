📚 Grammar Practice

Choose a level that matches your current English and gradually work towards more advanced grammar.

A1 — Beginner

1. Choose the correct sentence:

A) She are a student.
B) She is a student.
C) She am a student.
D) She be a student.
Answer: B — She is a student.
2. Complete the sentence:

I ___ two brothers.

A) has
B) having
C) have
D) had
Answer: C — have.

A2 — Elementary

3. Choose the correct form:

We ___ to the park yesterday.

A) go
B) goes
C) went
D) going
Answer: C — went.
4. Complete the sentence:

There ___ some milk in the fridge.

A) are
B) is
C) be
D) were
Answer: B — is.

B1 — Intermediate

5. Choose the correct conditional:

If it rains tomorrow, we ___ at home.

A) stayed
B) will stay
C) would stayed
D) staying
Answer: B — will stay.
6. Choose the correct form:

She has ___ her homework.

A) finish
B) finished
C) finishing
D) finishes
Answer: B — finished.

B2 — Upper Intermediate

7. Choose the correct form:

If I ___ more time, I would learn another language.

A) have
B) had
C) will have
D) having
Answer: B — had.
8. Choose the correct sentence:

A) Despite of the rain, we went outside.
B) Despite the rain, we went outside.
C) Despite it was raining, we went outside.
D) Despite the rain was heavy, we went outside.
Answer: B — Despite the rain, we went outside.

C1 — Advanced

9. Choose the correct form:

Had she known about the problem, she ___ earlier.

A) would act
B) would have acted
C) will act
D) acted
Answer: B — would have acted.
10. Choose the correct sentence:

A) Rarely I have encountered such a difficult problem.
B) Rarely have I encountered such a difficult problem.
C) Rarely encountered I have such a difficult problem.
D) Have rarely I encountered such a difficult problem.
Answer: B — Rarely have I encountered such a difficult problem.

C2 — Proficiency

11. Choose the best completion:

No sooner ___ the announcement than the audience began to react.

A) had he made
B) he had made
C) did he made
D) he made had
Answer: A — had he made.
12. Choose the most appropriate form:

It is essential that every applicant ___ the instructions carefully.

A) reads
B) read
C) reading
D) has read
Answer: B — read.

🔤 Vocabulary Practice

A1 Vocabulary

Happy

Small
Meaning: Not large in size.

A2 Vocabulary

Journey
Meaning: The act of travelling from one place to another.
Healthy
Meaning: In good health.

B1 Vocabulary

Reliable
Meaning: Able to be trusted or depended on.
Opportunity
Meaning: A favourable situation or chance.

B2 Vocabulary

Significant
Meaning: Important or large enough to be noticed.
Compelling
Meaning: Very convincing or interesting.

C1 Vocabulary

Meticulous
Meaning: Very careful and precise about details.
Inevitable
Meaning: Certain to happen and impossible or very difficult to avoid.

C2 Vocabulary

Ambiguous
Meaning: Open to more than one interpretation.
Ubiquitous
Meaning: Present or found almost everywhere.

🗣️ Pronunciation Practice

Focus on sounds, word stress and differences between similar words.

1. Knife

The letter "k" is silent.

2. Honest

The letter "h" is silent.

3. Ship / Sheep

These words contain different vowel sounds.

4. Record

The pronunciation and stress can differ depending on whether it is used as a noun or a verb.

5. Comfortable

In natural English speech, some syllables may be pronounced less strongly than learners expect from the spelling.

🧠 Mixed English Practice

13. Which word is closest in meaning to "brief"?

A) Short
B) Heavy
C) Loud
D) Ancient
Answer: A — Short.
14. Choose the correct sentence:

A) They has finished their work.
B) They have finished their work.
C) They having finished their work.
D) They is finished their work.
Answer: B — They have finished their work.
15. Which linking word normally introduces a contrast?

A) However
B) Therefore
C) Furthermore
D) Similarly
Answer: A — However.
16. Which sentence is most suitable for formal writing?

A) Give me the details now.
B) I wanna know the details.
C) Could you please provide the requested details?
D) Tell me everything.
Answer: C — Could you please provide the requested details?
