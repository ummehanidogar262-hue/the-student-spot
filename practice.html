🧠 English Practice

Test your vocabulary, grammar, writing and pronunciation skills.

📖 Vocabulary

1. What does "meticulous" mean?

A) Careless
B) Very careful and precise
C) Extremely fast
D) Confused
2. What is closest in meaning to "substantial"?

A) Tiny
B) Significant
C) Temporary
D) Unusual
3. What does "inevitable" mean?

A) Impossible to avoid
B) Easy to change
C) Difficult to understand
D) Completely unexpected

📚 Grammar

4. Choose the correct answer:

If I ______ harder, I would have passed the examination.

A) studied
B) had studied
C) have studied
D) would study
5. Choose the correct sentence:

A) Rarely I have seen such a beautiful place.
B) Rarely have I seen such a beautiful place.
C) Rarely I saw have such a beautiful place.
D) Have rarely I seen such a beautiful place.
6. Choose the correct answer:

She ______ have forgotten about the meeting.

A) must
B) must to
C) must have
D) must has

✍️ Writing

7. Which is most appropriate for a formal email?

A) Hey! Tell me about the course.
B) I wanna know about the course.
C) I would be grateful if you could provide further information.
D) Give me the information.
8. Which linking word shows contrast?

A) Furthermore
B) Therefore
C) However
D) Consequently

🗣️ Pronunciation

9. Which word contains a silent "k"?

A) Keep
B) Kind
C) Knife
D) Kitchen
10. Which pair is a minimal pair?

A) Ship / sheep
B) Book / books
C) Teacher / student
D) Quickly / slowly

✅ Answer Key

1. B — Very careful and precise
2. B — Significant
3. A — Impossible to avoid
4. B — had studied
5. B — Rarely have I seen...
6. C — must have
7. C — I would be grateful if you could provide further information.
8. C — However
9. C — Knife
10. A — Ship / sheep
