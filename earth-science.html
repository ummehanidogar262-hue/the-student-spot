🌍 Earth Science

Explore our planet, rocks, minerals, atmosphere, oceans, weather and Earth processes.

📖 Quick Earth Science Guide

Earth science is the study of Earth, its materials, its structure, its atmosphere, its oceans and the natural processes that shape our planet.

Important idea:

Earth is a dynamic planet. Its rocks, landforms, atmosphere, oceans and living environments are constantly changing through natural processes.

Diagram 1 — Internal Structure of the Earth

Earth has several main internal layers: the crust, mantle, outer core and inner core. Each layer has different properties and composition.

Diagram 2 — The Rock Cycle

The rock cycle describes how rocks change from one type to another through processes such as melting, cooling, weathering, compaction, heat and pressure.

Diagram 3 — Water Cycle

The water cycle is the continuous movement of water between the atmosphere, land and oceans. Important processes include evaporation, condensation, precipitation and runoff.

Diagram 4 — Layers of the Atmosphere

Earth's atmosphere is divided into several layers. The troposphere is the lowest layer and contains most weather. The stratosphere contains the ozone layer.

Diagram 5 — Plate Tectonics

Earth's outer shell is divided into large tectonic plates. Their movement can cause earthquakes, volcanic activity, mountain building and other geological processes.

Diagram 6 — Structure of a Volcano

A volcano is an opening in Earth's crust through which magma, gases and volcanic material can reach the surface. When magma reaches the surface, it is called lava.

🧠 Earth Science — 55 Short Questions with Answers

1. What is Earth science?
Answer: Earth science is the study of Earth, its materials, structure and natural processes.
2. What is the shape of Earth?
Answer: Earth is approximately spherical, but it is slightly flattened at the poles.
3. What is the outermost solid layer of Earth?
Answer: The crust is the outermost solid layer of Earth.
4. What are the main layers of Earth?
Answer: The main layers are the crust, mantle, outer core and inner core.
5. What is the mantle?
Answer: The mantle is the thick layer of hot rock beneath Earth's crust.
6. What is the outer core?
Answer: The outer core is a very hot, mostly liquid layer surrounding the inner core.
7. What is the inner core?
Answer: The inner core is the extremely hot, dense central part of Earth.
8. What is a mineral?
Answer: A mineral is a naturally occurring inorganic solid with a definite chemical composition and crystal structure.
9. What is a rock?
Answer: A rock is a naturally occurring solid material made of one or more minerals or mineral-like substances.
10. What are the three main types of rocks?
Answer: The three main types are igneous, sedimentary and metamorphic rocks.
11. What is an igneous rock?
Answer: An igneous rock forms when magma or lava cools and solidifies.
12. What is a sedimentary rock?
Answer: A sedimentary rock forms from sediments that are deposited, compacted and cemented.
13. What is a metamorphic rock?
Answer: A metamorphic rock forms when an existing rock is changed by heat, pressure or both.
14. What is the rock cycle?
Answer: The rock cycle describes how rocks change from one type to another through geological processes.
15. What is weathering?
Answer: Weathering is the breakdown of rocks at or near Earth's surface.
16. What is erosion?
Answer: Erosion is the movement of weathered rock and soil by water, wind, ice or gravity.
17. What is deposition?
Answer: Deposition is the process by which transported sediment is dropped or laid down.
18. What is soil?
Answer: Soil is a mixture of mineral particles, organic matter, water, air and living organisms.
19. What is the water cycle?
Answer: The water cycle is the continuous movement of water between Earth's surface and the atmosphere.
20. What is evaporation?
Answer: Evaporation is the change of liquid water into water vapour.
21. What is condensation?
Answer: Condensation is the change of water vapour into liquid water.
22. What is precipitation?
Answer: Precipitation is water that falls from clouds as rain, snow, sleet or hail.
23. What is runoff?
Answer: Runoff is water that flows over the land surface toward rivers, lakes or oceans.
24. What is groundwater?
Answer: Groundwater is water stored beneath Earth's surface in soil and rock.
25. What is the atmosphere?
Answer: The atmosphere is the layer of gases surrounding Earth.
26. Which gas is most abundant in Earth's atmosphere?
Answer: Nitrogen is the most abundant gas in Earth's atmosphere.
27. Which gas is needed by humans for respiration?
Answer: Humans need oxygen for respiration.
28. What is the troposphere?
Answer: The troposphere is the lowest layer of the atmosphere and contains most weather.
29. What is the stratosphere?
Answer: The stratosphere is the atmospheric layer above the troposphere and contains the ozone layer.
30. What is the ozone layer?
Answer: The ozone layer is a region of the stratosphere containing relatively high concentrations of ozone that absorbs much of the Sun's harmful ultraviolet radiation.
31. What is weather?
Answer: Weather is the short-term condition of the atmosphere at a particular place and time.
32. What is climate?
Answer: Climate is the long-term pattern of weather in a region.
33. What is temperature?
Answer: Temperature is a measure of how hot or cold something is.
34. What is air pressure?
Answer: Air pressure is the force exerted by the weight of the atmosphere on a surface.
35. What is wind?
Answer: Wind is moving air, generally moving from areas of higher pressure toward areas of lower pressure.
36. What is humidity?
Answer: Humidity is the amount of water vapour present in the air.
37. What causes day and night?
Answer: Day and night are caused by Earth's rotation on its axis.
38. What causes a year on Earth?
Answer: A year is caused by Earth's revolution around the Sun.
39. What is Earth's axis?
Answer: Earth's axis is an imaginary line passing through the North and South Poles around which Earth rotates.
40. What is a tectonic plate?
Answer: A tectonic plate is a large rigid piece of Earth's lithosphere that moves over the softer material beneath it.
41. What is plate tectonics?
Answer: Plate tectonics is the scientific theory that Earth's lithosphere is divided into moving plates.
42. What is an earthquake?
Answer: An earthquake is the sudden shaking of the ground caused by the release of energy in Earth's crust or upper mantle.
43. What is a volcano?
Answer: A volcano is an opening in Earth's crust through which magma, gases and volcanic materials can reach the surface.
44. What is magma?
Answer: Magma is molten rock located beneath Earth's surface.
45. What is lava?
Answer: Lava is molten rock that reaches Earth's surface.
46. What is a fault?
Answer: A fault is a fracture or zone of fractures in Earth's crust along which movement can occur.
47. What is a tsunami?
Answer: A tsunami is a series of large sea waves usually caused by a sudden displacement of seawater, often from an undersea earthquake.
48. What is a continent?
Answer: A continent is one of Earth's large continuous landmasses.
49. What is an ocean?
Answer: An ocean is a vast body of salt water covering a large part of Earth's surface.
50. What is a glacier?
Answer: A glacier is a large, long-lasting mass of ice that flows slowly over land.
51. What is a fossil?
Answer: A fossil is preserved evidence of past life, such as remains, impressions or traces in rocks.
52. How are fossils usually formed?
Answer: Fossils commonly form when remains or traces of organisms are buried and preserved in sediment over long periods.
53. What is a natural resource?
Answer: A natural resource is a useful material or substance obtained from nature.
54. What is renewable energy?
Answer: Renewable energy comes from sources that are naturally replenished, such as sunlight, wind and flowing water.
55. Why is Earth science important?
Answer: Earth science helps us understand our planet, natural resources, weather, climate, natural hazards and changes to Earth's environment.
