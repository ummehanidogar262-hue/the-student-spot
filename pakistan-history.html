🇵🇰 Pakistan History

Explore the people, movements and important events that shaped Pakistan.

📖 About Pakistan History

Pakistan's history includes ancient civilizations, the period of British rule, political movements, the Pakistan Movement, independence and the development of the country after 1947.

This page contains important people and 65 short-answer questions for easy student revision.

⭐ Important People of Pakistan History

Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah

Founder of Pakistan and the country's first Governor-General. He led the All-India Muslim League during the final phase of the Pakistan Movement.

Allama Muhammad Iqbal

Poet, philosopher and political thinker whose ideas strongly influenced Muslim political thought in the subcontinent.

Fatima Jinnah

Sister and close companion of Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah. She played an important role in the Pakistan Movement and later became a prominent political figure.

Sir Syed Ahmad Khan

Educational reformer who promoted modern education among Muslims of the Indian subcontinent and founded the Muhammadan Anglo-Oriental College at Aligarh.

📚 65 Pakistan History Questions with Answers

1. What is Pakistan?
Answer: Pakistan is a country in South Asia that became independent on 14 August 1947.
2. When did Pakistan become independent?
Answer: Pakistan became independent on 14 August 1947.
3. Who was the founder of Pakistan?
Answer: Muhammad Ali Jinnah, known as Quaid-e-Azam, was the founder of Pakistan.
4. When was Muhammad Ali Jinnah born?
Answer: Muhammad Ali Jinnah was born on 25 December 1876 in Karachi.
5. When did Muhammad Ali Jinnah die?
Answer: Muhammad Ali Jinnah died on 11 September 1948.
6. What was Jinnah's profession?
Answer: Jinnah was a lawyer, politician and statesman.
7. What does Quaid-e-Azam mean?
Answer: Quaid-e-Azam means "Great Leader."
8. What was the All-India Muslim League?
Answer: It was a political organization founded to represent Muslim political interests in British India.
9. When was the All-India Muslim League founded?
Answer: The All-India Muslim League was founded in 1906.
10. Where was the Muslim League founded?
Answer: It was founded at a political meeting in Dacca, now Dhaka, in 1906.
11. Who was Allama Muhammad Iqbal?
Answer: Allama Iqbal was a poet, philosopher and political thinker whose ideas influenced the Pakistan Movement.
12. When was Allama Iqbal born?
Answer: Allama Iqbal was born on 9 November 1877 in Sialkot.
13. When did Allama Iqbal die?
Answer: Allama Iqbal died on 21 April 1938 in Lahore.
14. What was Allama Iqbal's profession?
Answer: He was a poet, philosopher, lawyer and political thinker.
15. What was the Allahabad Address?
Answer: It was Allama Iqbal's presidential address to the Muslim League session at Allahabad in 1930.
16. What idea did Iqbal present in his Allahabad Address?
Answer: He discussed a political future for Muslims in the north-western parts of the Indian subcontinent.
17. Who was Sir Syed Ahmad Khan?
Answer: Sir Syed Ahmad Khan was an educational reformer, scholar and writer who promoted modern education among Muslims.
18. When was Sir Syed Ahmad Khan born?
Answer: Sir Syed Ahmad Khan was born in Delhi in 1817.
19. What was the Aligarh Movement?
Answer: It was a reform movement associated with Sir Syed Ahmad Khan that promoted modern education and social reform.
20. What college did Sir Syed establish at Aligarh?
Answer: He established the Muhammadan Anglo-Oriental College at Aligarh.
21. Who was Fatima Jinnah?
Answer: Fatima Jinnah was the sister and close companion of Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah.
22. What title is commonly associated with Fatima Jinnah?
Answer: She is commonly known as Madar-e-Millat, meaning Mother of the Nation.
23. What was the Pakistan Movement?
Answer: It was the political movement that sought a separate homeland for Muslims of British India.
24. What was the Lahore Resolution?
Answer: It was a resolution adopted by the Muslim League in Lahore in 1940 concerning independent states in Muslim-majority areas.
25. When was the Lahore Resolution passed?
Answer: It was passed on 23 March 1940.
26. Where was the Lahore Resolution passed?
Answer: It was passed at the Muslim League's Lahore session.
27. Who presented the Lahore Resolution?
Answer: A. K. Fazlul Huq presented the resolution at the Lahore session.
28. Why was the Lahore Resolution important?
Answer: It became a major milestone in the political struggle that led to the creation of Pakistan.
29. What was the Two-Nation Theory?
Answer: It was the political idea that Muslims and Hindus of British India constituted distinct nations with different religious and social identities.
30. What was the Lucknow Pact?
Answer: It was a 1916 agreement between the Indian National Congress and the Muslim League on constitutional and political demands.
31. What was the Khilafat Movement?
Answer: It was a political movement in British India that sought to support the Ottoman Caliphate after the First World War.
32. What was the Government of India Act 1935?
Answer: It was a major British constitutional law that introduced provincial autonomy and proposed a federal structure for British India.
33. What were the provincial elections of 1937?
Answer: They were elections held under the Government of India Act 1935 that resulted in Congress ministries forming governments in several provinces.
34. What was the Cabinet Mission Plan?
Answer: It was a 1946 British proposal intended to arrange a constitutional settlement for British India.
35. What was the 3 June Plan?
Answer: It was the 1947 plan announced by the British government for the partition of British India and the transfer of power.
36. What was the Indian Independence Act 1947?
Answer: It was the British law that created the independent dominions of India and Pakistan.
37. When did Pakistan's Constituent Assembly first meet?
Answer: Its first session was held on 10 August 1947 in Karachi.
38. When was Jinnah elected President of the Constituent Assembly?
Answer: Jinnah was unanimously elected President of the Constituent Assembly on 11 August 1947.
39. When did Pakistan become independent?
Answer: Pakistan became independent on 14 August 1947.
40. Who was Pakistan's first Governor-General?
Answer: Muhammad Ali Jinnah was Pakistan's first Governor-General.
41. Who was Pakistan's first Prime Minister?
Answer: Liaquat Ali Khan was Pakistan's first Prime Minister.
42. What was the Objectives Resolution?
Answer: It was a resolution adopted by Pakistan's Constituent Assembly in 1949 that laid down principles for the future constitutional system.
43. When was the Objectives Resolution passed?
Answer: It was passed on 12 March 1949.
44. What was Pakistan's first constitution?
Answer: Pakistan's first constitution was the Constitution of 1956.
45. When did Pakistan become a republic?
Answer: Pakistan became a republic on 23 March 1956.
46. What happened in Pakistan in 1958?
Answer: Martial law was imposed in October 1958 and the 1956 Constitution was abrogated.
47. What was the Constitution of 1962?
Answer: It was a constitution introduced during the rule of President Ayub Khan.
48. What happened in the 1965 war between Pakistan and India?
Answer: Pakistan and India fought a major war in 1965, followed by a ceasefire.
49. What was the Tashkent Declaration?
Answer: It was an agreement signed by Pakistan and India in January 1966 to end the 1965 conflict and restore relations.
50. What happened in 1971?
Answer: East Pakistan became the independent country of Bangladesh after the Bangladesh Liberation War.
51. Who became Prime Minister after the 1971 crisis?
Answer: Zulfikar Ali Bhutto became President in December 1971 and later Prime Minister under the 1973 constitutional system.
52. What is the Constitution of 1973?
Answer: It is Pakistan's current constitutional framework, adopted in 1973.
53. When did the Constitution of 1973 come into force?
Answer: It came into force on 14 August 1973.
54. Who was Zulfikar Ali Bhutto?
Answer: Zulfikar Ali Bhutto was a Pakistani political leader who served as President and Prime Minister.
55. Who was Liaquat Ali Khan?
Answer: Liaquat Ali Khan was a leading figure in the Pakistan Movement and became Pakistan's first Prime Minister.
56. What was the Simla Agreement?
Answer: It was an agreement signed by Pakistan and India in 1972 following the 1971 war.
57. What was the Pakistan nuclear programme?
Answer: It was Pakistan's long-term effort to develop nuclear technology, including a nuclear weapons capability.
58. When did Pakistan conduct its nuclear tests?
Answer: Pakistan conducted nuclear tests in May 1998.
59. Where were Pakistan's 1998 nuclear tests conducted?
Answer: The tests were conducted at the Chagai testing site in Balochistan.
60. What was the Kargil conflict?
Answer: It was a military conflict between India and Pakistan in the Kargil region in 1999.
61. Why is 14 August important in Pakistan?
Answer: It is Pakistan's Independence Day.
62. Why is 23 March important in Pakistan?
Answer: It marks Pakistan Day and commemorates the Lahore Resolution of 1940; it is also associated with the 1956 Constitution.
63. Why is 9 November remembered in Pakistan?
Answer: It is observed as the birth anniversary of Allama Muhammad Iqbal.
64. Why is 25 December important in Pakistan?
Answer: It is the birth anniversary of Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah and is observed as a national day.
65. Why is learning Pakistan's history important?
Answer: It helps students understand the country's origins, important leaders, political movements and major events.
