🌍 Earth

Explore our planet, its layers, movements, continents and oceans.

📖 What is Earth?

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only planet currently known to support life. It has land, water, an atmosphere and a variety of living organisms.

Earth is made up of different layers and has many natural features, including mountains, valleys, rivers, oceans, deserts and forests.

Important fact: About 71% of Earth's surface is covered by water, while about 29% is covered by land.

🌎 Diagram 1 — Earth and Its Main Features

Earth's surface contains large areas of land called continents and large bodies of salt water called oceans.

🌋 Diagram 2 — Layers of the Earth

Earth has four main layers: the crust, mantle, outer core and inner core. These layers differ in composition, temperature and physical properties.

🔄 Diagram 3 — Earth's Rotation

Earth rotates on its axis. One complete rotation takes about 24 hours and produces day and night.

☀️ Diagram 4 — Earth's Revolution Around the Sun

Earth revolves around the Sun. One complete revolution takes approximately 365¼ days, which makes one year.

🧭 Diagram 5 — Latitude

Latitude measures distance north or south of the Equator. The Equator is at 0° latitude.

🧭 Diagram 6 — Longitude

Longitude measures distance east or west of the Prime Meridian. The Prime Meridian is 0° longitude.

🌎 Diagram 7 — Seven Continents

The seven continents are Asia, Africa, North America, South America, Antarctica, Europe and Australia.

🌊 Diagram 8 — Five Oceans

The five oceans are the Pacific, Atlantic, Indian, Southern and Arctic Oceans.

💧 Diagram 9 — Water Cycle

The water cycle describes the continuous movement of water between Earth's surface and atmosphere through processes such as evaporation, condensation, precipitation and collection.

🪨 Diagram 10 — Rock Cycle

Rocks can change from one type to another through processes such as melting, cooling, heat, pressure, weathering and erosion.

🧠 Earth Science Practice — 60 Short Questions with Answers

1. What is Earth?
Answer: Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the planet on which we live.
2. What is Earth's natural satellite?
Answer: The Moon is Earth's natural satellite.
3. What is Earth's shape?
Answer: Earth is approximately spherical, although it is slightly flattened at the poles.
4. How much of Earth's surface is covered by water?
Answer: About 71% of Earth's surface is covered by water.
5. What is a continent?
Answer: A continent is one of Earth's large continuous land areas.
6. How many continents are commonly recognised?
Answer: Seven continents are commonly recognised.
7. Name the seven continents.
Answer: Asia, Africa, Europe, North America, South America, Australia and Antarctica.
8. Which is the largest continent?
Answer: Asia is the largest continent by area.
9. Which is the smallest continent?
Answer: Australia is the smallest continent by area in the commonly used seven-continent model.
10. What is an ocean?
Answer: An ocean is a very large body of salt water that covers part of Earth's surface.
11. How many oceans are commonly recognised?
Answer: Five oceans are commonly recognised.
12. Name the five oceans.
Answer: Pacific, Atlantic, Indian, Southern and Arctic Oceans.
13. Which is the largest ocean?
Answer: The Pacific Ocean is the largest ocean.
14. What is the Equator?
Answer: The Equator is an imaginary line around Earth at 0° latitude.
15. What does latitude measure?
Answer: Latitude measures distance north or south of the Equator.
16. What is the Prime Meridian?
Answer: The Prime Meridian is the imaginary line at 0° longitude.
17. What does longitude measure?
Answer: Longitude measures distance east or west of the Prime Meridian.
18. What is the Northern Hemisphere?
Answer: It is the half of Earth north of the Equator.
19. What is the Southern Hemisphere?
Answer: It is the half of Earth south of the Equator.
20. What is Earth's axis?
Answer: Earth's axis is an imaginary line passing through the North and South Poles.
21. What is rotation?
Answer: Rotation is the spinning of Earth around its axis.
22. How long does Earth take to rotate once?
Answer: Earth takes about 24 hours to complete one rotation.
23. What causes day and night?
Answer: Earth's rotation causes day and night.
24. What is revolution?
Answer: Revolution is the movement of Earth around the Sun.
25. How long does Earth take to orbit the Sun?
Answer: Earth takes about 365¼ days to complete one orbit.
26. What is a year?
Answer: A year is the approximately 365¼-day period Earth takes to complete one revolution around the Sun.
27. Why do we have a leap year?
Answer: A leap year helps account for the extra quarter of a day in Earth's yearly orbit.
28. What is the atmosphere?
Answer: The atmosphere is the layer of gases surrounding Earth.
29. Which gas is most abundant in Earth's atmosphere?
Answer: Nitrogen is the most abundant gas in Earth's atmosphere.
30. What is the crust?
Answer: The crust is Earth's thin, outermost solid layer.
31. What is the mantle?
Answer: The mantle is the thick layer beneath Earth's crust.
32. What is the outer core?
Answer: The outer core is a very hot layer of Earth's interior made mainly of liquid metal.
33. What is the inner core?
Answer: The inner core is Earth's extremely hot, dense central layer and is mainly solid.
34. What is a rock?
Answer: A rock is a naturally occurring solid material made of one or more minerals or mineraloids.
35. What are the three main types of rock?
Answer: The three main types are igneous, sedimentary and metamorphic rocks.
36. What is an igneous rock?
Answer: An igneous rock forms when molten rock cools and solidifies.
37. What is a sedimentary rock?
Answer: A sedimentary rock forms from sediments that are deposited, compacted and cemented.
38. What is a metamorphic rock?
Answer: A metamorphic rock forms when an existing rock is changed by heat, pressure or both.
39. What is weathering?
Answer: Weathering is the breakdown of rocks at or near Earth's surface.
40. What is erosion?
Answer: Erosion is the movement of weathered material from one place to another.
41. What is a mineral?
Answer: A mineral is a naturally occurring inorganic solid with a characteristic chemical composition and structure.
42. What is soil?
Answer: Soil is a mixture of mineral particles, organic matter, water, air and living organisms.
43. What is the water cycle?
Answer: The water cycle is the continuous movement of water between Earth's surface and the atmosphere.
44. What is evaporation?
Answer: Evaporation is the change of liquid water into water vapour.
45. What is condensation?
Answer: Condensation is the change of water vapour into liquid water.
46. What is precipitation?
Answer: Precipitation is water that falls from the atmosphere to Earth's surface as rain, snow, sleet or hail.
47. What is a river?
Answer: A river is a natural flowing body of water that usually moves toward a lake, sea or another river.
48. What is a mountain?
Answer: A mountain is a high area of land that rises prominently above its surroundings.
49. What is a valley?
Answer: A valley is a low area of land between hills or mountains, often containing a river.
50. What is a desert?
Answer: A desert is a very dry region that receives little precipitation.
51. What is a volcano?
Answer: A volcano is an opening or structure through which magma, gases and volcanic material can reach Earth's surface.
52. What is an earthquake?
Answer: An earthquake is the shaking of Earth's surface caused by the sudden release of energy within Earth.
53. What is a tectonic plate?
Answer: A tectonic plate is a large, rigid section of Earth's lithosphere that moves slowly.
54. What happens when tectonic plates move?
Answer: Their movement can cause earthquakes, volcanic activity and the formation of mountains and other landforms.
55. What is weather?
Answer: Weather is the condition of the atmosphere at a particular place and time.
56. What is climate?
Answer: Climate is the long-term pattern of weather in a particular region.
57. What is temperature?
Answer: Temperature is a measure of how hot or cold something is.
58. What is precipitation?
Answer: Precipitation is water falling from clouds to Earth's surface.
59. What is a natural resource?
Answer: A natural resource is a useful material or feature obtained from nature.
60. Why is Earth important to humans?
Answer: Earth provides humans with air, water, food, land and natural resources needed for life.
