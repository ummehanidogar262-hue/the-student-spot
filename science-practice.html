🧠 100 Short-Answer Questions with Answers

BIOLOGY 1. What is biology?
Answer: Biology is the study of living organisms.
BIOLOGY 2. What is the basic unit of life?
Answer: The cell is the basic unit of life.
BIOLOGY 3. What is the function of the nucleus?
Answer: The nucleus controls cell activities and contains genetic material.
BIOLOGY 4. What does the cell membrane do?
Answer: It controls what enters and leaves the cell.
BIOLOGY 5. What is cytoplasm?
Answer: Cytoplasm is the jelly-like material where many chemical reactions occur.
BIOLOGY 6. What is the function of mitochondria?
Answer: Mitochondria release energy from food during respiration.
BIOLOGY 7. What is the function of the cell wall?
Answer: The cell wall supports and strengthens a plant cell.
BIOLOGY 8. What do chloroplasts do?
Answer: Chloroplasts are the main sites of photosynthesis in plant cells.
BIOLOGY 9. What is a vacuole?
Answer: A vacuole is a cell structure that stores substances such as cell sap in plant cells.
BIOLOGY 10. What is photosynthesis?
Answer: Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants use light energy to make glucose from carbon dioxide and water.
BIOLOGY 11. Which gas is needed for photosynthesis?
Answer: Carbon dioxide.
BIOLOGY 12. Which gas is released during photosynthesis?
Answer: Oxygen.
BIOLOGY 13. What is chlorophyll?
Answer: Chlorophyll is the green pigment that absorbs light energy for photosynthesis.
BIOLOGY 14. What is respiration?
Answer: Respiration is the process that releases energy from food.
BIOLOGY 15. What is the function of the respiratory system?
Answer: It brings oxygen into the body and removes carbon dioxide.
BIOLOGY 16. What is the trachea?
Answer: The trachea is the main airway that carries air toward the lungs.
BIOLOGY 17. What are alveoli?
Answer: Alveoli are tiny air sacs where gas exchange occurs.
BIOLOGY 18. What is the function of the heart?
Answer: The heart pumps blood around the body.
BIOLOGY 19. What are arteries?
Answer: Arteries are blood vessels that carry blood away from the heart.
BIOLOGY 20. What are veins?
Answer: Veins are blood vessels that carry blood toward the heart.
BIOLOGY 21. What are capillaries?
Answer: Capillaries are tiny blood vessels where substances are exchanged with tissues.
BIOLOGY 22. What is digestion?
Answer: Digestion is the breakdown of food into smaller substances that can be absorbed.
BIOLOGY 23. Where does digestion begin?
Answer: Digestion begins in the mouth.
BIOLOGY 24. What is the function of the small intestine?
Answer: It completes much of digestion and absorbs nutrients.
BIOLOGY 25. What is a food chain?
Answer: A food chain shows how energy passes between organisms through feeding.
BIOLOGY 26. What is a producer?
Answer: A producer is an organism that makes its own food, usually by photosynthesis.
BIOLOGY 27. What is a consumer?
Answer: A consumer gets its food by eating other organisms.
BIOLOGY 28. What is a herbivore?
Answer: A herbivore mainly eats plants.
BIOLOGY 29. What is a carnivore?
Answer: A carnivore mainly eats other animals.
BIOLOGY 30. What is an ecosystem?
Answer: An ecosystem consists of organisms interacting with one another and their physical environment.
CHEMISTRY 31. What is chemistry?
Answer: Chemistry is the study of substances, their properties and the changes they undergo.
CHEMISTRY 32. What is matter?
Answer: Matter is anything that has mass and occupies space.
CHEMISTRY 33. What are the three common states of matter?
Answer: Solid, liquid and gas.
CHEMISTRY 34. What is a solid?
Answer: A solid has a fixed shape and a fixed volume.
CHEMISTRY 35. What is a liquid?
Answer: A liquid has a fixed volume but takes the shape of its container.
CHEMISTRY 36. What is a gas?
Answer: A gas has no fixed shape or fixed volume.
CHEMISTRY 37. What is an atom?
Answer: An atom is the smallest unit of an element that retains the properties of that element.
CHEMISTRY 38. What is a molecule?
Answer: A molecule is a group of two or more atoms chemically bonded together.
CHEMISTRY 39. What is an element?
Answer: An element is a pure substance made of only one type of atom.
CHEMISTRY 40. What is a compound?
Answer: A compound is a substance made from two or more different elements chemically combined.
CHEMISTRY 41. What is a mixture?
Answer: A mixture contains two or more substances that are not chemically combined.
CHEMISTRY 42. What is a physical change?
Answer: A physical change changes the form or state of a substance without producing a new substance.
CHEMISTRY 43. What is a chemical change?
Answer: A chemical change produces one or more new substances.
CHEMISTRY 44. What is melting?
Answer: Melting is the change from a solid to a liquid.
CHEMISTRY 45. What is freezing?
Answer: Freezing is the change from a liquid to a solid.
CHEMISTRY 46. What is evaporation?
Answer: Evaporation is the change of a liquid into a gas at its surface.
CHEMISTRY 47. What is condensation?
Answer: Condensation is the change of a gas into a liquid.
CHEMISTRY 48. What is an acid?
Answer: An acid is a substance that has acidic properties and produces hydrogen ions in aqueous solution.
CHEMISTRY 49. What is a base?
Answer: A base is a substance that can neutralise an acid.
CHEMISTRY 50. What is neutralisation?
Answer: Neutralisation is a reaction between an acid and a base that produces salt and water.
CHEMISTRY 51. What is the pH scale used for?
Answer: The pH scale is used to indicate how acidic or alkaline a solution is.
CHEMISTRY 52. What does a pH below 7 generally indicate?
Answer: It generally indicates an acidic solution.
CHEMISTRY 53. What does a pH of 7 indicate?
Answer: It indicates a neutral solution under standard conditions.
CHEMISTRY 54. What does a pH above 7 generally indicate?
Answer: It generally indicates an alkaline solution.
CHEMISTRY 55. What is a chemical reaction?
Answer: A chemical reaction is a process in which substances are changed into new substances.
CHEMISTRY 56. What are reactants?
Answer: Reactants are the substances present at the start of a chemical reaction.
CHEMISTRY 57. What are products?
Answer: Products are the new substances formed during a chemical reaction.
CHEMISTRY 58. What is an indicator?
Answer: An indicator is a substance that changes colour depending on whether a solution is acidic or alkaline.
CHEMISTRY 59. What is filtration?
Answer: Filtration separates an insoluble solid from a liquid.
CHEMISTRY 60. What is distillation?
Answer: Distillation separates substances using differences in boiling points.
EARTH SCIENCE 61. What is Earth science?
Answer: Earth science is the study of Earth, its materials, processes and systems.
EARTH SCIENCE 62. What are the main layers of Earth?
Answer: The crust, mantle, outer core and inner core.
EARTH SCIENCE 63. What is the crust?
Answer: The crust is Earth's thin outer solid layer.
EARTH SCIENCE 64. What is the mantle?
Answer: The mantle is the thick layer beneath Earth's crust.
EARTH SCIENCE 65. What is the outer core?
Answer: The outer core is a very hot liquid layer mainly composed of iron and nickel.
EARTH SCIENCE 66. What is the inner core?
Answer: The inner core is the extremely hot, solid central part of Earth.
EARTH SCIENCE 67. What is a rock?
Answer: A rock is a naturally occurring solid material made of one or more minerals or mineraloids.
EARTH SCIENCE 68. What are the three main types of rock?
Answer: Igneous, sedimentary and metamorphic rocks.
EARTH SCIENCE 69. How are igneous rocks formed?
Answer: They form when molten rock cools and solidifies.
EARTH SCIENCE 70. How are sedimentary rocks formed?
Answer: They form from sediments that are deposited, compacted and cemented.
EARTH SCIENCE 71. How are metamorphic rocks formed?
Answer: They form when existing rocks are changed by heat, pressure or both.
EARTH SCIENCE 72. What is the rock cycle?
Answer: The rock cycle describes how rocks can change from one type into another over time.
EARTH SCIENCE 73. What is weathering?
Answer: Weathering is the breakdown of rocks at Earth's surface.
EARTH SCIENCE 74. What is erosion?
Answer: Erosion is the movement of weathered rock and soil by agents such as water, wind or ice.
EARTH SCIENCE 75. What is a mineral?
Answer: A mineral is a naturally occurring inorganic solid with a characteristic chemical composition and crystal structure.
EARTH SCIENCE 76. What is the water cycle?
Answer: The water cycle is the continuous movement of water between Earth's surface, atmosphere and underground stores.
EARTH SCIENCE 77. What is evaporation?
Answer: Evaporation changes liquid water into water vapour.
EARTH SCIENCE 78. What is condensation?
Answer: Condensation changes water vapour into liquid water.
EARTH SCIENCE 79. What is precipitation?
Answer: Precipitation is water that falls from clouds as rain, snow, sleet or hail.
EARTH SCIENCE 80. What is groundwater?
Answer: Groundwater is water stored below Earth's surface in soil and rock.
EARTH SCIENCE 81. What is the atmosphere?
Answer: The atmosphere is the layer of gases surrounding Earth.
EARTH SCIENCE 82. What is weather?
Answer: Weather is the short-term condition of the atmosphere at a particular place and time.
EARTH SCIENCE 83. What is climate?
Answer: Climate is the long-term pattern of weather in a region.
EARTH SCIENCE 84. What causes wind?
Answer: Wind is caused by differences in air pressure, which result from uneven heating of Earth's surface.
EARTH SCIENCE 85. What is humidity?
Answer: Humidity is the amount of water vapour in the air.
EARTH SCIENCE 86. What is a cloud?
Answer: A cloud is a visible collection of tiny water droplets and/or ice crystals in the atmosphere.
EARTH SCIENCE 87. What is an earthquake?
Answer: An earthquake is the shaking of Earth's surface caused by the sudden release of energy in the crust.
EARTH SCIENCE 88. What is a volcano?
Answer: A volcano is an opening in Earth's crust through which molten rock, gases or ash can reach the surface.
EARTH SCIENCE 89. What are tectonic plates?
Answer: Tectonic plates are large pieces of Earth's lithosphere that move slowly over the asthenosphere.
EARTH SCIENCE 90. What can happen when tectonic plates move?
Answer: Their movement can cause earthquakes, volcanoes and the formation of mountains.
EARTH SCIENCE 91. What is a fossil?
Answer: A fossil is preserved evidence or remains of ancient life.
EARTH SCIENCE 92. What is soil?
Answer: Soil is a mixture of mineral particles, organic matter, water, air and living organisms.
EARTH SCIENCE 93. Why is soil important?
Answer: Soil supports plant growth and provides habitats and nutrients for many organisms.
EARTH SCIENCE 94. What is a natural resource?
Answer: A natural resource is a useful material or feature obtained from nature.
EARTH SCIENCE 95. Give one example of a renewable resource.
Answer: Solar energy is a renewable resource.
EARTH SCIENCE 96. Give one example of a non-renewable resource.
Answer: Coal is a non-renewable resource.
EARTH SCIENCE 97. What is pollution?
Answer: Pollution is the introduction of harmful substances or energy into the environment.
EARTH SCIENCE 98. What is conservation?
Answer: Conservation means protecting and using natural resources responsibly.
EARTH SCIENCE 99. Why is the Sun important to Earth?
Answer: The Sun provides energy that drives many Earth systems and supports life.
EARTH SCIENCE 100. Why is Earth science important?
Answer: Earth science helps us understand Earth's materials, natural processes, resources and environment.
