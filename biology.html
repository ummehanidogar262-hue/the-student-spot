📖 Quick Biology Guide

Biology is the study of living organisms and how they grow, survive, reproduce and interact with their environment.

Important idea:

The cell is the basic structural and functional unit of life. Different cells have different structures and functions.

🔬 Diagram 1 — Animal Cell

Nucleus
Cytoplasm
Mitochondria
Cell Membrane

Nucleus: controls the activities of the cell.
Cytoplasm: where many chemical reactions occur.
Mitochondria: release energy from food.
Cell membrane: controls what enters and leaves the cell.
