🧬 Biology

Learn about cells, plants, animals, the human body and life processes.

📖 Biology — Quick Introduction

Biology is the branch of science that studies living organisms. It helps us understand plants, animals, microorganisms and the human body.

Important idea:
All living organisms are made of cells. Cells are the basic structural and functional units of life.

🧠 Biology — 55 Short Questions with Answers

1. What is biology?
Answer: Biology is the branch of science that studies living organisms.
2. What is the basic unit of life?
Answer: The cell is the basic unit of life.
3. What is the function of the nucleus?
Answer: The nucleus controls many activities of the cell and contains genetic material.
4. What is the function of the cell membrane?
Answer: The cell membrane controls the movement of substances into and out of the cell.
5. What is cytoplasm?
Answer: Cytoplasm is the jelly-like material in a cell where many chemical reactions occur.
6. What is the function of mitochondria?
Answer: Mitochondria are the sites of aerobic respiration and release energy for the cell.
7. What is the function of the cell wall?
Answer: The cell wall supports the plant cell and helps it maintain its shape.
8. What is the function of chloroplasts?
Answer: Chloroplasts contain chlorophyll and are the sites of photosynthesis.
9. What is the function of the large vacuole?
Answer: The large vacuole contains cell sap and helps maintain pressure inside the plant cell.
10. Give one difference between a plant cell and an animal cell.
Answer: A plant cell has a cell wall and chloroplasts, while an animal cell does not.
11. What is photosynthesis?
Answer: Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants use light energy to make glucose from carbon dioxide and water.
12. Which gas is needed for photosynthesis?
Answer: Carbon dioxide is needed for photosynthesis.
13. Which gas is released during photosynthesis?
Answer: Oxygen is released during photosynthesis.
14. What is chlorophyll?
Answer: Chlorophyll is the green pigment that absorbs light energy for photosynthesis.
15. Why do plants need light?
Answer: Plants need light energy to carry out photosynthesis.
16. What are the raw materials of photosynthesis?
Answer: Carbon dioxide and water are the raw materials of photosynthesis.
17. What food is produced during photosynthesis?
Answer: Glucose is produced during photosynthesis.
18. What is respiration?
Answer: Respiration is the process by which cells release energy from food.
19. What is the main function of the respiratory system?
Answer: Its main function is to obtain oxygen and remove carbon dioxide from the body.
20. What is the trachea?
Answer: The trachea is a tube that carries air from the throat towards the bronchi.
21. What are bronchi?
Answer: The bronchi are the two main branches of the trachea that carry air into the lungs.
22. What are alveoli?
Answer: Alveoli are tiny air sacs in the lungs where gas exchange occurs.
23. What happens during gas exchange?
Answer: Oxygen moves from the alveoli into the blood, while carbon dioxide moves from the blood into the alveoli.
24. What is the function of the heart?
Answer: The heart pumps blood around the body.
25. What is the circulatory system?
Answer: The circulatory system consists of the heart, blood and blood vessels and transports substances around the body.
26. What are arteries?
Answer: Arteries are blood vessels that carry blood away from the heart.
27. What are veins?
Answer: Veins are blood vessels that carry blood towards the heart.
28. What are capillaries?
Answer: Capillaries are tiny blood vessels where substances are exchanged between blood and tissues.
29. What do red blood cells transport?
Answer: Red blood cells mainly transport oxygen around the body.
30. Why is blood important?
Answer: Blood transports oxygen, nutrients, hormones and waste substances around the body.
31. What is digestion?
Answer: Digestion is the breakdown of large food molecules into smaller soluble substances that can be absorbed.
32. Where does digestion begin?
Answer: Digestion begins in the mouth.
33. What is the function of the stomach?
Answer: The stomach stores and mixes food and begins the digestion of proteins.
34. What is the function of the small intestine?
Answer: The small intestine completes much of digestion and absorbs digested nutrients.
35. What is the function of the large intestine?
Answer: The large intestine absorbs water from undigested food and helps form faeces.
36. What is a nutrient?
Answer: A nutrient is a substance needed by the body for energy, growth, repair or normal functioning.
37. Why does the body need proteins?
Answer: Proteins are needed mainly for growth and repair of body tissues.
38. What is a food chain?
Answer: A food chain shows how energy is transferred from one organism to another through feeding.
39. What is a producer?
Answer: A producer is an organism that makes its own food, usually by photosynthesis.
40. What is a consumer?
Answer: A consumer is an organism that obtains food by eating other organisms.
41. What is a herbivore?
Answer: A herbivore is an animal that mainly eats plants.
42. What is a carnivore?
Answer: A carnivore is an animal that mainly eats other animals.
43. What is an omnivore?
Answer: An omnivore eats both plant material and animal material.
44. What are decomposers?
Answer: Decomposers break down dead organisms and return nutrients to the environment.
45. What is a habitat?
Answer: A habitat is the place where an organism normally lives.
46. What is an ecosystem?
Answer: An ecosystem consists of living organisms interacting with one another and with their physical environment.
47. What is an adaptation?
Answer: An adaptation is a feature that helps an organism survive and reproduce in its environment.
48. What is reproduction?
Answer: Reproduction is the biological process by which organisms produce offspring.
49. Why is reproduction important?
Answer: Reproduction allows organisms to produce new individuals and continue their species.
50. What is an organ?
Answer: An organ is a structure made of different tissues working together to perform a particular function.
51. What is an organ system?
Answer: An organ system is a group of organs working together to perform one or more important functions.
52. What is the function of the nervous system?
Answer: The nervous system receives information and coordinates responses in the body.
53. What is the skeletal system?
Answer: The skeletal system is the framework of bones that supports and protects the body.
54. What is the difference between living and non-living things?
Answer: Living things perform life processes such as nutrition, respiration, growth and reproduction, while non-living things do not.
55. Why is biology important?
Answer: Biology helps us understand living organisms, the human body, health, plants, animals and the environment.
