🏙️ Human Geography

Explore people, population, settlements, migration, cities and human activities.

📚 What is Human Geography?

Human geography is the study of people, their activities, settlements and relationships with places and the environment.

Important idea:

Physical geography mainly studies natural features of Earth, while human geography focuses on people and how they use and organize space.
👨‍👩‍👧 Population — where people live and how populations change.
🏠 Settlements — villages, towns and cities where people live.
🚶 Migration — movement of people from one place to another.
🏙️ Urbanization — growth of towns and cities.
🌾 Agriculture — growing crops and raising animals.
💼 Economic activities — work people do to earn a living.

Diagram 1 — Population Distribution

Population distribution describes how people are spread across an area. Some places are densely populated, while others are sparsely populated.

Diagram 2 — Population Density

Population density describes the number of people living in a particular unit of area, often measured as people per square kilometre.

Diagram 3 — Rural and Urban Settlements

Rural settlements are generally associated with countryside, farming and smaller communities. Urban settlements include towns and cities with more buildings, services and infrastructure.

Diagram 4 — Migration

Migration is the movement of people from one place to another. Push factors encourage people to leave, while pull factors attract people to another place.

Diagram 5 — Urbanization

Urbanization is the process in which an increasing proportion of people live in towns and cities. It is often associated with movement from rural areas to urban areas.

Diagram 6 — Economic Activities

Economic activities can be grouped into sectors. Primary activities obtain resources, secondary activities process materials, and tertiary activities provide services. Quaternary activities include knowledge and information-based work.

Diagram 7 — Human and Environment Interaction

People depend on the environment for resources such as water, food and land. At the same time, human activities can change the natural environment.

🧠 Human Geography — 55 Short Questions with Answers

1. What is human geography?
Answer: Human geography is the study of people, their activities, settlements and relationships with places and the environment.
2. What does population mean?
Answer: Population means the number of people living in a particular area.
3. What is population distribution?
Answer: Population distribution describes how people are spread across an area.
4. What is population density?
Answer: Population density is the number of people living in a unit area.
5. What is a densely populated area?
Answer: It is an area where many people live in a relatively small area.
6. What is a sparsely populated area?
Answer: It is an area where relatively few people live over a large area.
7. Name one factor affecting population distribution.
Answer: Climate is one factor that affects population distribution.
8. How can water affect population distribution?
Answer: Reliable water supplies can attract settlements and support human activities.
9. How can fertile soil affect population distribution?
Answer: Fertile soil can support agriculture and encourage people to settle in an area.
10. How can relief affect settlement?
Answer: Flat or gently sloping land is often easier for building, transport and farming.
11. What is a settlement?
Answer: A settlement is a place where people live.
12. What is a rural settlement?
Answer: A rural settlement is a settlement in the countryside, often associated with farming and smaller communities.
13. What is an urban settlement?
Answer: An urban settlement is a town or city with a relatively large population and developed infrastructure.
14. What is a village?
Answer: A village is a small settlement, often found in rural areas.
15. What is a town?
Answer: A town is a settlement larger and generally more developed than a village.
16. What is a city?
Answer: A city is a large urban settlement containing many people, buildings, services and economic activities.
17. What is urbanization?
Answer: Urbanization is the process in which an increasing proportion of people live in towns and cities.
18. Why do people move to cities?
Answer: People may move to cities for employment, education, healthcare and other services.
19. What is migration?
Answer: Migration is the movement of people from one place to another.
20. What is internal migration?
Answer: Internal migration is movement from one place to another within the same country.
21. What is international migration?
Answer: International migration is movement from one country to another.
22. What is a push factor?
Answer: A push factor is a condition that encourages people to leave a place.
23. What is a pull factor?
Answer: A pull factor is a condition that attracts people to a new place.
24. Give one example of a push factor.
Answer: Lack of employment can be a push factor.
25. Give one example of a pull factor.
Answer: Better employment opportunities can be a pull factor.
26. What is immigration?
Answer: Immigration is entering a country to live there.
27. What is emigration?
Answer: Emigration is leaving a country to live in another country.
28. What is population growth?
Answer: Population growth is an increase in the number of people in a population.
29. What causes natural population increase?
Answer: Natural population increase occurs when the birth rate is higher than the death rate.
30. What is birth rate?
Answer: Birth rate is the number of live births in a population during a specified period, usually expressed per 1,000 people per year.
31. What is death rate?
Answer: Death rate is the number of deaths in a population during a specified period, usually expressed per 1,000 people per year.
32. What is life expectancy?
Answer: Life expectancy is the average number of years a person is expected to live under current conditions.
33. What is a population census?
Answer: A population census is an official count and collection of information about people living in an area.
34. Why is a census useful?
Answer: Census information helps governments plan services, infrastructure and resources.
35. What is population structure?
Answer: Population structure describes the characteristics of a population, such as age and sex composition.
36. What is an age structure?
Answer: Age structure shows how a population is divided among different age groups.
37. Why is age structure important?
Answer: Age structure helps planners understand the population's needs for schools, jobs, healthcare and other services.
38. What is an economic activity?
Answer: An economic activity is work involved in producing goods or providing services.
39. What is the primary sector?
Answer: The primary sector obtains raw materials and natural resources from the environment.
40. Give two examples of primary activities.
Answer: Farming and fishing are examples of primary activities.
41. What is the secondary sector?
Answer: The secondary sector processes raw materials and manufactures goods.
42. Give one example of a secondary activity.
Answer: Manufacturing is an example of a secondary activity.
43. What is the tertiary sector?
Answer: The tertiary sector provides services to people and businesses.
44. Give two examples of tertiary activities.
Answer: Teaching and banking are examples of tertiary activities.
45. What is the quaternary sector?
Answer: The quaternary sector includes knowledge, information, research and technology-based activities.
46. How does transport affect human geography?
Answer: Transport connects places, supports trade and makes movement of people and goods easier.
47. How does technology affect human geography?
Answer: Technology changes communication, transportation, employment and the way people use places.
48. What is human-environment interaction?
Answer: Human-environment interaction describes how people depend on, adapt to and modify their environment.
49. Why do people depend on the environment?
Answer: People depend on the environment for resources such as water, food, land and raw materials.
50. How can humans modify the environment?
Answer: Humans can modify the environment through activities such as building cities, farming and constructing roads.
51. What is a natural resource?
Answer: A natural resource is a useful material or feature obtained from the natural environment.
52. What is sustainable development?
Answer: Sustainable development aims to meet present needs while protecting resources and opportunities for future generations.
53. What is a megacity?
Answer: A megacity is a very large urban area with a population of at least 10 million people.
54. Why is human geography important?
Answer: Human geography helps us understand population, settlements, migration, economic activities and relationships between people and places.
55. How can human geography help planners?
Answer: It provides information that can help planners make decisions about housing, transport, services, resources and land use.
