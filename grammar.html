📖 English Grammar

Simple grammar lessons for students 💜

🔤 What is Grammar?

Grammar is the set of rules we use to arrange words correctly and form meaningful sentences.

Example:
Sara reads a book.

🧩 Nouns

A noun is a word that names a person, place, animal, thing or idea.

Examples:
teacher • school • cat • book • happiness

🏃 Verbs

A verb usually describes an action or a state.

Examples:
run • write • eat • learn • is

✨ Adjectives

An adjective describes or gives more information about a noun.

Examples:
beautiful flower • clever student • big house

⏰ Basic Tenses

Tenses help us understand when an action happens.

Present: I study every day.

Past: I studied yesterday.

Future: I will study tomorrow.

📝 Quick Practice

Question:

Identify the verb in this sentence:

The students study English.

✅ Answer: study
