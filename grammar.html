📖 C1 English Grammar

Understand advanced grammar and use it confidently.

1. Advanced Conditionals

Conditionals describe situations and their possible results. At C1 level, you should be comfortable with mixed and inverted conditionals.

Mixed conditional:
If + past perfect → would + base verb
Example:
If I had studied harder last year, I would understand this topic much better now.
Another example:
If she weren't so busy, she would have attended the event yesterday.

2. Inversion

In formal English, we can place certain negative or limiting expressions at the beginning of a sentence and invert the subject and auxiliary verb.

Normal:
I had never seen such a beautiful place.

Inverted:
Never had I seen such a beautiful place.
Examples:
Rarely do students get such an opportunity.
Not only did she finish the project, but she also presented it.

3. Modal Verbs of Deduction

Modal verbs can express how certain we are about a past or present situation.

Present:
must be → strong certainty
might be → possibility
can't be → strong belief that something is impossible

Past:
must have + past participle
might have + past participle
can't have + past participle
Example:
She isn't answering her phone. She must be busy.

They left an hour ago, so they must have arrived by now.

4. Participle Clauses

Participle clauses allow us to combine information and make writing more concise.

Example:
Having finished her homework, Maria went outside.

Instead of:
Maria had finished her homework, so she went outside.
Another example:
Feeling tired, he decided to take a break.

5. Cleft Sentences

Cleft sentences allow us to emphasise particular information.

Structure:
It + be + emphasised information + that/who...
Example:
It was Sarah who solved the problem.

The emphasis is on Sarah.

6. Relative Clauses

Relative clauses provide additional information about a noun. At advanced level, you should understand defining, non-defining and reduced relative clauses.

Example:
The book, which was published last year, became extremely popular.
Reduced form:
Students studying abroad often develop greater independence.

7. Advanced Passive Structures

Passive structures are especially common in formal, academic and scientific writing.

Example:
The researchers are believed to have discovered a new species.
Another example:
The problem is thought to have been caused by a technical error.

8. Reported Speech

Reported speech is used to communicate what someone said without repeating their exact words.

Direct:
"I am studying for my exam."

Reported:
She said that she was studying for her exam.
Advanced example:
He claimed that he had never been informed about the changes.

9. Wish & Regret

We use different structures to talk about present situations, past regrets and desired changes.

Present:
I wish I had more time.

Past regret:
I wish I had studied harder.

Annoying repeated behaviour:
I wish you wouldn't interrupt me.

10. Emphasis with Auxiliary Verbs

We can use do, does or did to emphasise an affirmative statement.

Example:
I do understand your point.

She did complete the assignment on time.
⚠️ Common mistake:
Do not use "did" with another past-tense verb.

❌ She did completed the work.
✅ She did complete the work.

🧠 C1 Grammar Practice

  1. If I ______ harder last year, I would be more confident now.

    A) studied
    B) had studied
    C) would study
    D) have studied
  2. Rarely ______ such an impressive performance.

    A) I have seen
    B) have I seen
    C) I saw
    D) saw I
  3. She isn't answering. She ______ be asleep.

    A) must
    B) should have
    C) would
    D) can't have
  4. ______ his homework, he went out with his friends.

    A) Finished
    B) Having finished
    C) Has finished
    D) Finish
  5. It was the teacher ______ explained the concept.

    A) which
    B) whose
    C) who
    D) where
  6. The scientist is believed ______ a major discovery.

    A) make
    B) making
    C) to have made
    D) made
  7. I wish I ______ more carefully for the examination.

    A) study
    B) studied
    C) had studied
    D) would study
  8. She ______ complete the assignment before the deadline.

    A) did
    B) did to
    C) did completed
    D) does completed

✅ Answer Key

1. B — had studied
2. B — have I seen
3. A — must
4. B — Having finished
5. C — who
6. C — to have made
7. C — had studied
8. A — did
