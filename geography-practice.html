📚 Geography Quick Guide

Geography is the study of Earth, its physical features, environments, people, places and the relationships between humans and the natural world.

Earth: The planet on which we live, including its layers, landforms, rocks, atmosphere and natural systems.
Weather & Climate: Weather describes atmospheric conditions at a particular time, while climate describes the usual pattern of weather over a long period.
Oceans & Water: This area of geography studies oceans, seas, rivers, lakes, groundwater, the water cycle and Earth's water resources.
Human Geography: The study of people, population, settlements, cities, migration, culture and human activities across Earth.

🌎 Part 1 — Earth

Crust: The thin outer solid layer of Earth.
Mantle: The thick layer beneath the crust.
Core: The deepest part of Earth.
Landform: A natural feature of Earth's surface.
Mountain: A high natural elevation of Earth's surface.
Plateau: A raised area of relatively level land.
Plain: A broad area of mostly flat land.
Valley: A low area between hills or mountains.

🧠 150 Geography Short Questions with Answers

1. What is geography?
Answer: Geography is the study of Earth's places, environments, physical features and people.
2. What is Earth?
Answer: Earth is the planet on which we live.
3. What is the crust?
Answer: The crust is Earth's thin outer solid layer.
4. What is the mantle?
Answer: The mantle is the thick layer beneath Earth's crust.
5. What is the core?
Answer: The core is the deepest part of Earth.
6. What is a landform?
Answer: A landform is a natural feature of Earth's surface.
7. What is a mountain?
Answer: A mountain is a high natural elevation of land.
8. What is a plateau?
Answer: A plateau is a raised area of relatively level land.
9. What is a plain?
Answer: A plain is a broad area of mostly flat land.
10. What is a valley?
Answer: A valley is a low area between hills or mountains.
11. What is a volcano?
Answer: A volcano is an opening through which magma, gases or ash can reach Earth's surface.
12. What is an earthquake?
Answer: An earthquake is the shaking of Earth's surface caused by movement within the Earth.
13. What are tectonic plates?
Answer: Tectonic plates are large moving pieces of Earth's lithosphere.
14. What is weathering?
Answer: Weathering is the breakdown of rocks at or near Earth's surface.
15. What is erosion?
Answer: Erosion is the movement of weathered material by agents such as water, wind or ice.
16. What is deposition?
Answer: Deposition is the laying down of transported sediment.
17. What is a continent?
Answer: A continent is a large continuous area of land.
18. What is an ocean?
Answer: An ocean is a very large body of salt water.
19. What is a map?
Answer: A map is a representation of a place or area.
20. What is a map scale?
Answer: Map scale shows the relationship between distance on a map and actual distance.
21. What is latitude?
Answer: Latitude measures distance north or south of the Equator.
22. What is longitude?
Answer: Longitude measures distance east or west of the Prime Meridian.
23. What is the Equator?
Answer: The Equator is the imaginary line at 0° latitude.
24. What is the Prime Meridian?
Answer: The Prime Meridian is the reference line at 0° longitude.
25. What is a hemisphere?
Answer: A hemisphere is one half of Earth.
26. What is a physical feature?
Answer: A physical feature is a natural feature of Earth's surface.
27. What is a natural resource?
Answer: A natural resource is something from nature that people can use.
28. What is a mineral?
Answer: A mineral is a naturally occurring inorganic substance with specific properties.
29. What is a rock?
Answer: A rock is a naturally occurring solid material made of one or more minerals.
30. Name the three major rock types.
Answer: Igneous, sedimentary and metamorphic rocks.
31. What is magma?
Answer: Magma is molten rock beneath Earth's surface.
32. What is lava?
Answer: Lava is molten rock that reaches Earth's surface.
33. What is a glacier?
Answer: A glacier is a large mass of slowly moving ice.
34. What is a desert?
Answer: A desert is a region with very low precipitation.
35. What is a coast?
Answer: A coast is the area where land meets a sea or ocean.
36. What is an island?
Answer: An island is land completely surrounded by water.
37. What is a peninsula?
Answer: A peninsula is land surrounded by water on three sides.
38. What is a strait?
Answer: A strait is a narrow waterway connecting larger bodies of water.
39. Why are mountains important?
Answer: Mountains influence climate, provide water sources and support ecosystems and human activities.
40. Why is Earth's surface important?
Answer: Earth's surface provides land, water and resources needed by living organisms and people.
41. What is weather?
Answer: Weather is the condition of the atmosphere at a particular time and place.
42. What is climate?
Answer: Climate is the usual pattern of weather in a place over a long period.
43. What is temperature?
Answer: Temperature measures how hot or cold the air is.
44. What is humidity?
Answer: Humidity is the amount of water vapour in the air.
45. What is precipitation?
Answer: Precipitation is water that falls from clouds to Earth's surface.
46. Name three forms of precipitation.
Answer: Rain, snow and hail.
47. What is wind?
Answer: Wind is moving air.
48. What causes wind?
Answer: Differences in air pressure cause air to move, producing wind.
49. What is air pressure?
Answer: Air pressure is the force exerted by the weight of air.
50. What is a cloud?
Answer: A cloud is a collection of tiny water droplets or ice crystals in the atmosphere.
51. What is evaporation?
Answer: Evaporation is the change of liquid water into water vapour.
52. What is condensation?
Answer: Condensation is the change of water vapour into liquid water.
53. What is the water cycle?
Answer: The water cycle is the continuous movement of water between Earth's surface and atmosphere.
54. What is a climate zone?
Answer: A climate zone is a region with similar long-term climate conditions.
55. What is a tropical climate?
Answer: A tropical climate is generally warm throughout the year.
56. What is a polar climate?
Answer: A polar climate is characterized by very cold conditions for much of the year.
57. What is a temperate climate?
Answer: A temperate climate generally has moderate temperatures and noticeable seasonal changes.
58. What is a drought?
Answer: A drought is a prolonged period of unusually low precipitation.
59. What is a flood?
Answer: A flood occurs when water covers land that is normally dry.
60. What is a storm?
Answer: A storm is a disturbed atmospheric condition that can involve strong winds and precipitation.
61. What is a thunderstorm?
Answer: A thunderstorm is a storm associated with thunder and lightning.
62. What is a hurricane?
Answer: A hurricane is a powerful tropical cyclone with very strong winds.
63. What is a tornado?
Answer: A tornado is a rapidly rotating column of air extending from a thunderstorm toward the ground.
64. Why does temperature vary?
Answer: Temperature varies because of factors such as latitude, altitude, season and distance from the sea.
65. How does altitude affect temperature?
Answer: Temperature generally decreases as altitude increases.
66. How does latitude affect climate?
Answer: Places at different latitudes receive different amounts of solar energy, affecting climate.
67. What is a weather forecast?
Answer: A weather forecast is a prediction of future atmospheric conditions.
68. Which instrument measures temperature?
Answer: A thermometer measures temperature.
69. Which instrument measures air pressure?
Answer: A barometer measures air pressure.
70. Which instrument measures wind speed?
Answer: An anemometer measures wind speed.
71. Which instrument measures rainfall?
Answer: A rain gauge measures rainfall.
72. Why are clouds important?
Answer: Clouds are important because they are involved in precipitation and influence Earth's temperature.
73. What is climate change?
Answer: Climate change is a long-term change in Earth's climate patterns.
74. What is the greenhouse effect?
Answer: The greenhouse effect is the warming of Earth's surface caused by gases in the atmosphere absorbing and re-emitting heat.
75. Why is weather important?
Answer: Weather affects agriculture, transport, water supplies, human activities and ecosystems.
76. What is an ocean?
Answer: An ocean is a vast body of salt water covering much of Earth's surface.
77. How many major oceans are commonly recognized?
Answer: Five major oceans are commonly recognized: Pacific, Atlantic, Indian, Southern and Arctic.
78. Which is the largest ocean?
Answer: The Pacific Ocean is the largest ocean.
79. What is a sea?
Answer: A sea is a large body of salt water that is generally smaller than an ocean and often partly enclosed by land.
80. What is a river?
Answer: A river is a natural flowing body of water.
81. What is a river source?
Answer: A river source is the place where a river begins.
82. What is a river mouth?
Answer: A river mouth is where a river enters a sea, ocean or lake.
83. What is a tributary?
Answer: A tributary is a smaller river or stream that flows into a larger river.
84. What is a drainage basin?
Answer: A drainage basin is the area of land drained by a river and its tributaries.
85. What is groundwater?
Answer: Groundwater is water stored beneath Earth's surface.
86. What is a lake?
Answer: A lake is a body of water surrounded by land.
87. What is an aquifer?
Answer: An aquifer is an underground layer of rock or sediment that can store and transmit groundwater.
88. What is ocean salinity?
Answer: Ocean salinity is the amount of dissolved salts in seawater.
89. What are ocean currents?
Answer: Ocean currents are large-scale movements of seawater.
90. Why are ocean currents important?
Answer: Ocean currents redistribute heat and influence marine environments and coastal climates.
91. What causes waves?
Answer: Most surface ocean waves are caused by wind transferring energy to the water.
92. What causes tides?
Answer: Tides are mainly caused by the gravitational effects of the Moon and the Sun.
93. What is a high tide?
Answer: High tide is the time when sea level reaches a higher position along a coast.
94. What is low tide?
Answer: Low tide is the time when sea level reaches a lower position along a coast.
95. Why are oceans important?
Answer: Oceans regulate climate, support ecosystems, provide resources and participate in the water cycle.
96. What is a coral reef?
Answer: A coral reef is a marine ecosystem built largely from the skeletons of corals.
97. What is marine life?
Answer: Marine life includes organisms that live in seas and oceans.
98. What is freshwater?
Answer: Freshwater is water containing very little dissolved salt.
99. Why is freshwater important?
Answer: Freshwater is essential for drinking, agriculture, ecosystems and many human activities.
100. What is a reservoir?
Answer: A reservoir is a natural or artificial storage area for water.
101. What is a dam?
Answer: A dam is a structure built across a river or valley to control or store water.
102. What is irrigation?
Answer: Irrigation is the artificial supply of water to crops.
103. What is water conservation?
Answer: Water conservation means using and managing water carefully to reduce waste.
104. What is water pollution?
Answer: Water pollution is the contamination of water by harmful substances.
105. What is desalination?
Answer: Desalination is the process of removing dissolved salts from seawater.
106. What is a delta?
Answer: A delta is an area of deposited sediment formed where a river enters a slower-moving body of water.
107. What is an estuary?
Answer: An estuary is a coastal area where freshwater from a river mixes with seawater.
108. What is a waterfall?
Answer: A waterfall is a place where river water drops steeply over a change in elevation.
109. What is a watershed?
Answer: A watershed is an area of land that drains water toward a common outlet.
110. Why should water resources be protected?
Answer: Water resources should be protected because clean water is essential for life, ecosystems and human activities.
111. What is human geography?
Answer: Human geography studies people, places, societies and their relationships with the environment.
112. What is population?
Answer: Population is the number of people living in a particular area.
113. What is population density?
Answer: Population density is the number of people living per unit area.
114. What is population distribution?
Answer: Population distribution describes how people are spread across an area.
115. What is migration?
Answer: Migration is the movement of people from one place to another to live temporarily or permanently.
116. What is immigration?
Answer: Immigration is moving into another country or place to live.
117. What is emigration?
Answer: Emigration is leaving a country or place to live somewhere else.
118. What is urbanization?
Answer: Urbanization is the growth of towns and cities and the increasing proportion of people living in urban areas.
119. What is a settlement?
Answer: A settlement is a place where people live.
120. What is a village?
Answer: A village is a small settlement, often associated with rural areas.
121. What is a city?
Answer: A city is a large and densely populated settlement with many services and economic activities.
122. What is a megacity?
Answer: A megacity is a very large urban area with a population of at least 10 million people.
123. What is a rural area?
Answer: A rural area is generally an area outside towns and cities with lower population density.
124. What is an urban area?
Answer: An urban area is a built-up area containing a relatively high concentration of people and services.
125. What is a push factor?
Answer: A push factor is a condition that encourages people to leave a place.
126. What is a pull factor?
Answer: A pull factor is a condition that attracts people to a place.
127. What is a natural increase?
Answer: Natural increase is the difference between the birth rate and death rate of a population.
128. What is birth rate?
Answer: Birth rate is the number of live births per population in a specified period, commonly expressed per 1,000 people per year.
129. What is death rate?
Answer: Death rate is the number of deaths per population in a specified period, commonly expressed per 1,000 people per year.
130. What is life expectancy?
Answer: Life expectancy is the average number of years a person is expected to live under current mortality conditions.
131. What is a census?
Answer: A census is an official count and collection of information about a population.
132. What is culture?
Answer: Culture includes the shared beliefs, customs, traditions, language and practices of a group.
133. What is a language?
Answer: A language is a system of communication used by people.
134. What is an economy?
Answer: An economy is the system through which goods and services are produced, distributed and consumed.
135. What is agriculture?
Answer: Agriculture is the cultivation of crops and the raising of animals for food and other products.
136. What is industry?
Answer: Industry involves producing goods or providing services through organized economic activity.
137. What is trade?
Answer: Trade is the buying and selling or exchange of goods and services.
138. What is transport?
Answer: Transport is the movement of people or goods from one place to another.
139. Why are roads important?
Answer: Roads connect places and help people and goods move between them.
140. What is a service?
Answer: A service is an activity provided to meet the needs of people or organizations.
141. What is development?
Answer: Development is improvement in economic, social and living conditions.
142. What is a renewable resource?
Answer: A renewable resource can naturally be replenished over time.
143. What is a non-renewable resource?
Answer: A non-renewable resource exists in limited quantities and forms very slowly.
144. What is sustainability?
Answer: Sustainability means using resources in ways that meet present needs without preventing future generations from meeting their needs.
145. What is environmental pollution?
Answer: Environmental pollution is the introduction of harmful substances or energy into the environment.
146. What is deforestation?
Answer: Deforestation is the removal or clearing of forests.
147. Why are forests important?
Answer: Forests provide habitats, resources, help regulate climate and protect soil and water systems.
148. What is conservation?
Answer: Conservation is the protection and careful management of natural resources and environments.
149. Why is geography useful?
Answer: Geography helps us understand Earth, people, environments, resources and how places are connected.
150. Why should students study geography?
Answer: Studying geography helps students understand the physical world, human societies and the relationship between people and their environment.
