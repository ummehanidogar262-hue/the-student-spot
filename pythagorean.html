📐 Pythagorean Theorem

Master right triangles and calculate missing sides.

📖 Quick Guide

The Pythagorean Theorem works with right-angled triangles. The longest side is called the hypotenuse.

Formula:

a² + b² = c²

Example:
a = 3, b = 4

3² + 4² = c²
9 + 16 = c²
25 = c²
c = 5

Remember

  • a and b are the two shorter sides.
  • c is always the hypotenuse.
  • The hypotenuse is opposite the right angle.
  • The theorem only applies to right-angled triangles.
