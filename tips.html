🎯 Study Tips

Simple ideas to make studying easier 💜

⏰ Make a Study Schedule

Choose a regular time for studying and include short breaks.

📵 Reduce Distractions

Keep unnecessary notifications and distractions away while studying.

📝 Take Short Notes

Write down the most important ideas in your own words.

🔄 Revise Regularly

Review your lessons regularly instead of waiting until the night before an exam.

💧 Take Care of Yourself

Get enough sleep, drink water, eat regular meals, and give yourself time to rest.
