🧠 Problem Solving

Think carefully, identify the information, and solve step by step.

📖 Problem-Solving Strategy

Good problem solving means understanding the question, identifying the useful information, choosing a method, solving carefully, and checking whether your answer makes sense.

Example:

A notebook costs $4. A student buys 6 notebooks. How much do they pay?

4 × 6 = 24

Answer: $24

Try these steps:

  1. Read the problem carefully.
  2. Identify what you know.
  3. Identify what you need to find.
  4. Choose the correct operation or formula.
  5. Solve the problem.
  6. Check your answer.
