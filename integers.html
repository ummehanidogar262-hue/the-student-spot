📖 Quick Integer Guide

Integers are whole numbers that can be positive, negative or zero. Examples include -8, -3, 0, 4 and 12.

Example:

(-7) + 12 = 5

When adding integers with different signs, subtract their absolute values and keep the sign of the number with the greater absolute value.

Important rules

  • Positive + positive = positive.
  • Negative + negative = negative.
  • Subtracting a number is the same as adding its opposite.
  • Positive × positive = positive.
  • Negative × negative = positive.
  • Positive × negative = negative.
  • The same sign rules apply to division.
