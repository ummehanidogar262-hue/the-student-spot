📝 Custom Notes

Create simple, organized and useful notes for your studies.

📚 What Are Custom Notes?

Custom notes are study notes that you organize in a way that makes sense to you. You can write important definitions, explanations, formulas, examples and key points.

Good notes make revision easier because they keep the most important information together in a simple format.

📖 Subjects You Can Make Notes For

📘 English ➗ Mathematics 🔬 Science 🌍 Geography 📜 History 💻 Computer

You can create notes for any subject you study. Keep each subject organized so that you can find information quickly.

✏️ How to Make Good Notes

  • Write the topic at the top.
  • Use short and clear sentences.
  • Highlight important words and definitions.
  • Use headings and subheadings.
  • Add examples when they help you understand the topic.
  • Use bullet points for long information.
  • Keep formulas and important facts easy to find.
  • Review your notes regularly.

💡 Example of a Good Note

Photosynthesis

Definition: Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants use light energy to make glucose from carbon dioxide and water.

Important Points:

  • It occurs mainly in green plants.
  • Light provides the energy.
  • Carbon dioxide is used.
  • Water is used.
  • Glucose is produced.
  • Oxygen is released.

🌟 Custom Notes Tips

  • Don't copy every sentence from a textbook.
  • Focus on information that helps you understand the topic.
  • Use your own words when possible.
  • Keep notes short enough for quick revision.
  • Review difficult topics more often.
