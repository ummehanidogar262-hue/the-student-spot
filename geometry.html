📖 Quick Geometry Guide

Geometry is the study of shapes, sizes, angles, distances and relationships between figures.

Rectangle example:

Length = 8 cm
Width = 5 cm

Area = length × width
Area = 8 × 5 = 40 cm²

Perimeter = 2(length + width)
Perimeter = 2(8 + 5) = 26 cm

Important formulas

  • Rectangle area = length × width
  • Rectangle perimeter = 2(length + width)
  • Square area = side × side
  • Square perimeter = 4 × side
  • Triangle area = 1/2 × base × height
  • Circle circumference = 2πr
  • Circle area = πr²
