🏛️ Important People in History

Learn about people who influenced ancient, medieval and modern history.

📚 About This Page

History is shaped by the actions, ideas, discoveries and leadership of many different people. This page introduces some important figures from different periods of world history.

👤 Six Important Historical Figures

Cleopatra VII

Cleopatra VII was the last active ruler of the Ptolemaic Kingdom of Egypt. She was known for her political ability and her role in the final period of ancient Egyptian history.

Historical artwork: Wikimedia Commons

Julius Caesar

Julius Caesar was a Roman general and statesman whose career played an important role in the transformation of the Roman Republic.

Photograph of historical bust: Wikimedia Commons

Alexander the Great

Alexander the Great was king of Macedon and created one of the largest empires of the ancient world through his military campaigns.

Ancient mosaic: Wikimedia Commons

Genghis Khan

Genghis Khan founded the Mongol Empire and united many Mongol tribes under his leadership.

Historical portrait: Wikimedia Commons

Napoleon Bonaparte

Napoleon Bonaparte was a French military leader and emperor whose actions greatly influenced European history in the early nineteenth century.

Historical portrait: Wikimedia Commons

Abraham Lincoln

Abraham Lincoln was the 16th president of the United States. He led the country during the American Civil War and issued the Emancipation Proclamation.

Historical photograph: Wikimedia Commons

🕰️ Simple Historical Timeline

Ancient World: Alexander the Great and Cleopatra VII
Roman World: Julius Caesar
Medieval Period: Genghis Khan
Modern History: Napoleon Bonaparte and Abraham Lincoln

🧠 55 Short Questions with Answers

1. Who was Cleopatra VII?
Answer: Cleopatra VII was the last active ruler of the Ptolemaic Kingdom of Egypt.
2. What kingdom did Cleopatra VII rule?
Answer: She ruled the Ptolemaic Kingdom of Egypt.
3. Why is Cleopatra important in history?
Answer: She was an influential ruler during the final period of ancient Egyptian history.
4. Who was Julius Caesar?
Answer: Julius Caesar was a Roman general and statesman.
5. What was Julius Caesar's role in Rome?
Answer: He was a powerful military leader and political figure in the late Roman Republic.
6. Why is Caesar important in Roman history?
Answer: His career contributed to major political changes in the Roman Republic.
7. Who was Alexander the Great?
Answer: Alexander the Great was king of Macedon and a famous ancient military leader.
8. What kingdom did Alexander rule?
Answer: He ruled the ancient kingdom of Macedon.
9. What did Alexander create?
Answer: He created a large empire through his military campaigns.
10. Why is Alexander important?
Answer: His conquests spread Greek influence across a large part of the ancient world.
11. Who was Genghis Khan?
Answer: Genghis Khan was the founder and first Great Khan of the Mongol Empire.
12. What empire did Genghis Khan establish?
Answer: He established the Mongol Empire.
13. How did Genghis Khan become powerful?
Answer: He united many Mongol tribes under his leadership.
14. Why is Genghis Khan important in history?
Answer: His leadership helped create one of the largest contiguous empires in history.
15. Who was Napoleon Bonaparte?
Answer: Napoleon Bonaparte was a French military leader and emperor.
16. What country did Napoleon lead?
Answer: Napoleon led France.
17. Why was Napoleon important?
Answer: His military campaigns and reforms had a major influence on European history.
18. What was the Napoleonic Code?
Answer: It was a civil law code introduced under Napoleon that influenced legal systems in many places.
19. Who was Abraham Lincoln?
Answer: Abraham Lincoln was the 16th president of the United States.
20. During which war was Lincoln president?
Answer: He was president during the American Civil War.
21. What was the Emancipation Proclamation?
Answer: It was a proclamation issued by Lincoln that declared enslaved people in the rebelling states to be free.
22. Why is Abraham Lincoln important?
Answer: He led the United States during the Civil War and played an important role in ending slavery.
23. What is a historical source?
Answer: A historical source is evidence that provides information about the past.
24. What is a primary source?
Answer: A primary source is evidence created during the period being studied.
25. Give one example of a primary source.
Answer: A letter written by a person during a historical event is a primary source.
26. What is a historical artifact?
Answer: An artifact is an object made or used by people in the past.
27. Why are ancient coins useful to historians?
Answer: They can provide evidence about rulers, governments, symbols and economies.
28. Why are ancient buildings important?
Answer: They provide evidence about the technology, culture and society of earlier civilizations.
29. What is an empire?
Answer: An empire is a group of territories or peoples controlled by one ruler or central authority.
30. What is a civilization?
Answer: A civilization is a complex human society with organized communities, institutions and culture.
31. Why were rivers important to early civilizations?
Answer: Rivers provided water, fertile land, transportation and resources.
32. What was ancient Egypt famous for?
Answer: Ancient Egypt was known for its organized society, writing, monumental architecture and Nile-based agriculture.
33. What was the Nile River's importance to Egypt?
Answer: The Nile provided water and helped make nearby land suitable for agriculture.
34. What were pyramids?
Answer: Pyramids were monumental structures built in ancient Egypt, including as royal tombs.
35. What was ancient Greece known for?
Answer: Ancient Greece was known for its city-states, philosophy, art, literature and political ideas.
36. What was Athens?
Answer: Athens was an important ancient Greek city-state.
37. What was Sparta?
Answer: Sparta was an ancient Greek city-state known for its military-oriented society.
38. What was ancient Rome?
Answer: Ancient Rome was a civilization that developed into a powerful republic and later an empire.
39. What was the Roman Republic?
Answer: The Roman Republic was the period when Rome was governed through republican institutions before the Roman Empire.
40. What was the Roman Empire?
Answer: The Roman Empire was the period when Rome controlled a vast territory under imperial government.
41. What was the Silk Road?
Answer: The Silk Road was a network of trade routes connecting parts of Asia, Europe and Africa.
42. Why was the Silk Road important?
Answer: It helped people exchange goods, ideas, technologies and cultures.
43. What was the Mongol Empire?
Answer: The Mongol Empire was a large empire founded by Genghis Khan and expanded by his successors.
44. What is the Middle Ages?
Answer: The Middle Ages was a period of European history between antiquity and the early modern period.
45. What is a monarch?
Answer: A monarch is a ruler such as a king or queen.
46. What is a dynasty?
Answer: A dynasty is a sequence of rulers from the same family or lineage.
47. What is a revolution?
Answer: A revolution is a major and often rapid change in a political or social system.
48. What is colonization?
Answer: Colonization is the establishment of control over another territory and its people.
49. Why do historians study leaders?
Answer: Leaders can influence governments, wars, laws, societies and major historical events.
50. Why are photographs useful in modern history?
Answer: Photographs can provide visual evidence about people, places and events.
51. Why can ancient people not usually have real photographs?
Answer: Photography was invented many centuries after most ancient historical figures lived.
52. What can a portrait tell historians?
Answer: A portrait can provide evidence about how a person was represented and how people wanted them to be remembered.
53. Why should historians compare different sources?
Answer: Comparing sources can help historians check evidence and identify different viewpoints.
54. What is historical evidence?
Answer: Historical evidence is information used to investigate and understand the past.
55. Why is studying important people useful?
Answer: It helps us understand how individuals influenced societies, governments, cultures and historical events.

🖼️ Important Note About the Images

For ancient people such as Cleopatra, Julius Caesar, Alexander the Great and Genghis Khan, no camera photograph from their lifetimes exists. The images above therefore use surviving historical portraits, busts, mosaics or other artwork.

Abraham Lincoln lived during the age of photography, so genuine historical photographs of him exist. Historical paintings and portraits are also used for people from periods before photography.
