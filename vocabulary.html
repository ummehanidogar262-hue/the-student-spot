📚 English Vocabulary

Build your English vocabulary from A1 beginner to C2 proficiency.

📖 What is Vocabulary?

Vocabulary means the words we know and use when we speak, read, write and understand a language.

Learning vocabulary at different levels helps students gradually move from simple everyday words to advanced academic and professional language.

💡 Student Tip: Learn a small number of new words regularly. Write the meaning, make your own sentence, and review the word later.

🟢 A1 — Beginner Vocabulary

A1 vocabulary focuses on simple everyday words used in basic conversations and familiar situations.

1. Family
Meaning: A group of related people.
Example: I live with my family.
2. Friend
Meaning: A person you like and know well.
Example: Sara is my best friend.
3. School
Meaning: A place where students learn.
Example: I go to school every morning.
4. Food
Meaning: Things that people or animals eat.
Example: My favourite food is rice.
5. Happy
Meaning: Feeling pleased or joyful.
Example: She is happy today.
6. Big
Meaning: Large in size.
Example: They live in a big house.
7. Small
Meaning: Little in size.
Example: I have a small bag.
8. Water
Meaning: A clear liquid that people need to live.
Example: Please drink some water.

🟡 A2 — Elementary Vocabulary

A2 vocabulary includes common words for everyday activities, experiences, places and simple descriptions.

1. Journey
Meaning: Travel from one place to another.
Example: The journey took three hours.
2. Advice
Meaning: An opinion about what someone should do.
Example: My teacher gave me good advice.
3. Decide
Meaning: To choose something after thinking about it.
Example: I decided to study tonight.
4. Improve
Meaning: To become better.
Example: I want to improve my English.
5. Healthy
Meaning: Good for your health.
Example: Fruit is part of a healthy diet.
6. Environment
Meaning: The natural world and surroundings around us.
Example: We should protect the environment.

🔵 B1 — Intermediate Vocabulary

B1 vocabulary helps students discuss experiences, opinions, plans, problems and familiar academic topics.

1. Achieve
Meaning: To successfully reach a goal.
Example: She worked hard to achieve her goal.
2. Challenge
Meaning: Something difficult that requires effort.
Example: Learning a language can be a challenge.
3. Opportunity
Meaning: A chance to do or achieve something.
Example: This course gives students a great opportunity.
4. Experience
Meaning: Knowledge gained through doing or seeing something.
Example: Travelling can be a valuable experience.
5. Responsible
Meaning: Having a duty to deal with something properly.
Example: Students should be responsible for their work.
6. Communicate
Meaning: To share information, thoughts or feelings.
Example: Good communication is important in teamwork.

🟣 B2 — Upper-Intermediate Vocabulary

B2 vocabulary allows students to express more detailed ideas, arguments, opinions and explanations.

1. Significant
Meaning: Important or noticeable.
Example: The discovery had a significant effect.
2. Influence
Meaning: The power to affect someone or something.
Example: Friends can influence our decisions.
3. Require
Meaning: To need something.
Example: This task requires careful planning.
4. Maintain
Meaning: To keep something at the same level or condition.
Example: Regular practice helps maintain your skills.
5. Approach
Meaning: A way of dealing with a situation or problem.
Example: We need a different approach to the problem.
6. Evidence
Meaning: Information that helps prove something.
Example: Scientists need evidence to support their conclusions.

🟠 C1 — Advanced Vocabulary

C1 vocabulary is useful for advanced reading, academic writing, formal discussions and detailed arguments.

1. Comprehensive
Meaning: Including everything necessary or important.
Example: The report provides a comprehensive analysis.
2. Nevertheless
Meaning: Despite what has just been said.
Example: The task was difficult; nevertheless, we completed it.
3. Imply
Meaning: To suggest something without saying it directly.
Example: His words seemed to imply that he disagreed.
4. Substantial
Meaning: Large in amount or important in effect.
Example: The project required a substantial amount of work.
5. Perspective
Meaning: A particular way of thinking about something.
Example: The book gives us a new perspective on history.
6. Allocate
Meaning: To officially give something for a particular purpose.
Example: Students should allocate enough time for revision.

🔴 C2 — Proficiency Vocabulary

C2 vocabulary contains highly advanced words used in sophisticated academic, literary, professional and formal communication.

1. Ambiguous
Meaning: Having more than one possible meaning or interpretation.
Example: The statement was too ambiguous to interpret confidently.
2. Meticulous
Meaning: Extremely careful and precise about details.
Example: She was meticulous when checking her research.
3. Coherent
Meaning: Logical, clear and well connected.
Example: He presented a coherent argument.
4. Inherent
Meaning: Existing naturally as part of something.
Example: Every method has inherent advantages and limitations.
5. Unprecedented
Meaning: Never having happened or existed before.
Example: The event created an unprecedented situation.
6. Substantiate
Meaning: To provide evidence that proves or supports something.
Example: Researchers must substantiate their claims with evidence.

🧠 How to Learn Vocabulary Effectively

✔ Learn words in context instead of memorising isolated words.

✔ Write your own example sentences.

✔ Review difficult words regularly.

✔ Group related words together.

✔ Read books, articles and stories to see vocabulary naturally.

✔ Use new words in your own speaking and writing.

⭐ Remember: You do not need to learn hundreds of words in one day. Consistent practice is more useful than memorising a huge list at once.
