🔤 C1 English Vocabulary

Build a sophisticated and precise vocabulary.

📚 How to use this resource

Advanced vocabulary is not simply about learning difficult words. Strong vocabulary means understanding meaning, tone, context, collocations and the subtle differences between similar words.

Study each word carefully, then complete the practice section without looking at the answers.

1. Alleviate

Meaning: To make something unpleasant, painful or difficult less severe.

Word type: Verb

Common collocations: alleviate pain, alleviate pressure, alleviate suffering, alleviate concerns

Example:
The new policy was introduced to alleviate financial pressure on low-income families.
Advanced example:
Although the treatment cannot eliminate the symptoms entirely, it can significantly alleviate their severity.

2. Ambiguous

Meaning: Having more than one possible interpretation or meaning, making something unclear.

Word type: Adjective

Common collocations: ambiguous statement, ambiguous wording, deliberately ambiguous, highly ambiguous

Example:
The instructions were so ambiguous that several students interpreted them differently.
Important distinction:
Ambiguous means unclear because there are multiple possible interpretations. It does not simply mean "difficult."

3. Coherent

Meaning: Logical, consistent and easy to understand because the ideas are well connected.

Word type: Adjective

Common collocations: coherent argument, coherent explanation, coherent structure, logically coherent

Example:
The student presented a coherent argument supported by relevant evidence.

4. Detrimental

Meaning: Causing harm or damage to something.

Word type: Adjective

Common collocations: detrimental effect, detrimental impact, detrimental to health, potentially detrimental

Example:
A lack of sleep can have a detrimental effect on concentration.
Useful structure:
"Something is detrimental to something."

5. Meticulous

Meaning: Extremely careful and precise, paying attention to even small details.

Word type: Adjective

Common collocations: meticulous planning, meticulous research, meticulous attention to detail

Example:
The researcher kept meticulous records throughout the experiment.

6. Substantial

Meaning: Large or significant in amount, size, importance or effect.

Word type: Adjective

Common collocations: substantial evidence, substantial increase, substantial amount, substantial improvement

Example:
The project required a substantial amount of time and research.

7. Inevitable

Meaning: Certain to happen and impossible or very difficult to avoid.

Word type: Adjective

Common collocations: inevitable consequence, inevitable outcome, seemingly inevitable

Example:
Given the circumstances, some degree of disagreement was inevitable.

🧩 Word Formation

Advanced vocabulary also involves recognising how words change their grammatical form.

Example:

analyse → analysis → analytical → analytically

decide → decision → decisive → decisively

significant → significance → significantly

Understanding word families helps you choose the correct form when writing sophisticated sentences.

📝 C1 Practice Questions

1. The government introduced several measures to ______ the effects of the economic crisis.

A) alleviate
B) aggravate
C) accumulate
D) allocate

2. The writer's argument was difficult to follow because it lacked a ______ structure.

A) detrimental
B) coherent
C) meticulous
D) substantial

3. Choose the correct word:

Excessive screen time may be ______ to children's concentration.

A) substantial
B) ambiguous
C) detrimental
D) inevitable

4. Which word best describes someone who pays extremely careful attention to detail?

A) coherent
B) meticulous
C) ambiguous
D) inevitable

✅ Answers

1. A — alleviate
2. B — coherent
3. C — detrimental
4. B — meticulous
