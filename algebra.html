📖 Quick Algebra Guide

Algebra uses letters to represent unknown values. The goal is often to find the value of the unknown.

Example:

3x + 5 = 20
3x = 15
x = 5

Important ideas

  • Collect like terms.
  • Use inverse operations.
  • Keep both sides of an equation balanced.
  • Check your answer by substituting it back.
