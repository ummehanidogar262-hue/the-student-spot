📖 Quick Percentage Guide

A percentage represents a number out of 100.

Example:

25% of 80

= 25/100 × 80
= 20

Important formulas

  • Percentage = (part ÷ whole) × 100
  • Part = percentage × whole
  • Percentage increase = increase ÷ original × 100
  • Percentage decrease = decrease ÷ original × 100
