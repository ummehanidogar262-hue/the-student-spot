📖 Quick Ratio Guide

A ratio compares two or more quantities. Ratios can be simplified by dividing all parts by their greatest common factor.

Example:

The ratio of boys to girls is 12 : 18.

Divide both parts by 6:
12 ÷ 6 : 18 ÷ 6
= 2 : 3

Simplified ratio = 2 : 3

Important ideas

  • Equivalent ratios have the same relationship.
  • Always use the same multiplier or divisor for every part.
  • Ratios can be written as 2 : 3, 2/3 or "2 to 3".
  • Unit rates compare a quantity to 1 unit.
