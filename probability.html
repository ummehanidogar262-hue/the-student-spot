🎲 Probability

Learn how to measure the likelihood of events.

📖 Quick Probability Guide

Probability tells us how likely an event is to happen. It ranges from 0 to 1, or from 0% to 100%.

Basic formula:

Probability = Number of favourable outcomes ÷ Total number of possible outcomes

Example:
A fair coin has 2 possible outcomes.

Probability of heads = 1/2 = 50%

Important ideas

  • Impossible event = probability 0.
  • Certain event = probability 1.
  • Probabilities can be written as fractions, decimals or percentages.
  • The total probability of all possible outcomes is 1.
