🌦️ Weather & Climate

Explore temperature, wind, humidity, rainfall, clouds, climate zones and weather events.

📚 Weather & Climate Guide

Weather describes the condition of the atmosphere at a particular place and time.

Climate describes the usual pattern of weather in a place over a long period of time.

Remember:

Weather can change from hour to hour or day to day, while climate describes long-term patterns.

🌡️ Temperature

Temperature tells us how hot or cold the air is. It is commonly measured with a thermometer in degrees Celsius (°C).

💨 Wind

Wind is moving air. Wind generally moves from areas of higher air pressure toward areas of lower air pressure.

💧 Humidity

Humidity is the amount of water vapour present in the air. Relative humidity is usually expressed as a percentage.

🌧️ Rainfall & Precipitation

Precipitation is water that falls from clouds to Earth's surface. It can include rain, snow, sleet or hail.

☁️ Clouds

Clouds form when moist air cools and water vapour condenses into tiny water droplets or ice crystals.

🌍 Climate Zones

Earth's major climate zones include tropical, temperate and polar regions. Climate varies with factors such as latitude, altitude and distance from the sea.

🌪️ Weather Events

Weather events include thunderstorms, hurricanes or tropical cyclones, tornadoes, floods, droughts and heatwaves.
