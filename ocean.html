📚 Quick Ocean & Water Guide

Water is one of Earth's most important natural resources. Oceans, seas, rivers, lakes, glaciers and groundwater are all parts of Earth's water system.

Important idea:

Most of Earth's surface is covered by water, and the oceans play an important role in Earth's climate, weather and ecosystems.

Diagram 1 — The Water Cycle

Water continuously moves between Earth's surface, atmosphere and underground through evaporation, condensation, precipitation, runoff and collection.

Diagram 2 — Ocean Depth Zones

Sunlight decreases as depth increases. Different ocean zones have different conditions and support different forms of life.

Diagram 3 — Ocean Wave

A wave is a moving disturbance on the surface of water. Waves have features such as a crest, trough, height and wavelength.

Diagram 4 — Tides and the Moon

Tides are regular rises and falls in sea level. The gravitational effects of the Moon and Sun contribute to tides.

Diagram 5 — Ocean Currents

Ocean currents are large-scale movements of seawater. They can transport heat and influence weather and climate.

Diagram 6 — Freshwater System

Freshwater occurs in glaciers and ice sheets, groundwater, lakes, rivers and other freshwater stores. It is essential for people, plants and animals.

Diagram 7 — Simple Water Treatment Process

Water treatment removes unwanted materials and helps make water suitable for its intended use.

🧠 Ocean & Water Practice — 55 Short Questions with Answers

1. What is an ocean?
Answer: An ocean is a vast body of salt water that covers a large part of Earth.
2. What is a sea?
Answer: A sea is a smaller body of salt water that is partly enclosed by land and connected to an ocean.
3. What is saltwater?
Answer: Saltwater is water containing dissolved salts and other minerals.
4. What is freshwater?
Answer: Freshwater is water with very low concentrations of dissolved salts.
5. Why are oceans important?
Answer: Oceans provide habitats, influence climate and weather, and support many human activities.
6. What is the water cycle?
Answer: The water cycle is the continuous movement of water between Earth's surface, atmosphere and underground.
7. What is evaporation?
Answer: Evaporation is the process in which liquid water changes into water vapour.
8. What is condensation?
Answer: Condensation is the process in which water vapour cools and changes into liquid water.
9. What is precipitation?
Answer: Precipitation is water that falls from clouds to Earth's surface as rain, snow, sleet or hail.
10. What is runoff?
Answer: Runoff is water that flows over the land surface toward rivers, lakes or oceans.
11. What is infiltration?
Answer: Infiltration is the movement of water from the surface into the soil.
12. What is groundwater?
Answer: Groundwater is water stored below Earth's surface in soil and rocks.
13. What is a glacier?
Answer: A glacier is a large mass of ice that forms on land and moves slowly.
14. What is a river?
Answer: A river is a natural stream of flowing water that usually moves toward a lake, sea or ocean.
15. What is a lake?
Answer: A lake is a body of water surrounded by land.
16. What is a reservoir?
Answer: A reservoir is a natural or artificial place where water is stored.
17. What is a wave?
Answer: A wave is a moving disturbance that travels across the surface of water.
18. What is a wave crest?
Answer: The crest is the highest part of a wave.
19. What is a wave trough?
Answer: The trough is the lowest part of a wave.
20. What is wavelength?
Answer: Wavelength is the distance between corresponding points on successive waves, such as two crests.
21. What is wave height?
Answer: Wave height is the vertical distance between a wave crest and its trough.
22. What causes many ocean surface waves?
Answer: Wind transferring energy to the water commonly produces ocean surface waves.
23. What are tides?
Answer: Tides are regular rises and falls in sea level.
24. What mainly causes ocean tides?
Answer: The gravitational effects of the Moon and the Sun are major causes of tides.
25. What is high tide?
Answer: High tide is the time when sea level reaches a relatively high position.
26. What is low tide?
Answer: Low tide is the time when sea level reaches a relatively low position.
27. What is an ocean current?
Answer: An ocean current is a large-scale movement of seawater in a particular direction.
28. Why are ocean currents important?
Answer: Ocean currents transport heat and can influence weather and climate.
29. What is a warm ocean current?
Answer: A warm ocean current carries relatively warm water from warmer regions toward cooler regions.
30. What is a cold ocean current?
Answer: A cold ocean current carries relatively cold water toward warmer regions.
31. What is salinity?
Answer: Salinity is the amount of dissolved salts in water.
32. Why is seawater salty?
Answer: Seawater contains dissolved salts and minerals that have accumulated through natural processes.
33. What is an estuary?
Answer: An estuary is a partly enclosed coastal area where freshwater from rivers mixes with seawater.
34. What is a coastline?
Answer: A coastline is the boundary between land and the sea.
35. What is a beach?
Answer: A beach is an area of sand, gravel or other loose material along a shore.
36. What is erosion?
Answer: Erosion is the wearing away and removal of rock or soil by agents such as water, wind or ice.
37. How can waves affect coastlines?
Answer: Waves can erode, transport and deposit material along coastlines.
38. What is a water resource?
Answer: A water resource is a source of water that can be used by people, ecosystems or other organisms.
39. Why is freshwater important?
Answer: Freshwater is essential for drinking, agriculture, ecosystems and many human activities.
40. What is water conservation?
Answer: Water conservation means using and managing water carefully to reduce unnecessary waste.
41. What is water pollution?
Answer: Water pollution occurs when harmful substances contaminate water and reduce its quality.
42. Name one source of water pollution.
Answer: Industrial waste can be a source of water pollution.
43. What is plastic pollution in oceans?
Answer: It is the accumulation of plastic waste in marine environments.
44. Why can ocean pollution be harmful?
Answer: Ocean pollution can damage marine habitats and harm aquatic organisms.
45. What is marine life?
Answer: Marine life refers to organisms that live in seas and oceans.
46. What is an aquatic ecosystem?
Answer: An aquatic ecosystem is a community of organisms interacting with each other and with their water environment.
47. What is a coral reef?
Answer: A coral reef is a marine ecosystem built mainly from structures produced by reef-building corals.
48. Why are coral reefs important?
Answer: Coral reefs provide habitats for many marine organisms and support diverse ecosystems.
49. What is the hydrosphere?
Answer: The hydrosphere includes all of Earth's water in oceans, rivers, lakes, groundwater, ice and the atmosphere.
50. What percentage of Earth's surface is covered by water?
Answer: About 71% of Earth's surface is covered by water.
51. Where is most of Earth's water found?
Answer: Most of Earth's water is found in the oceans as saltwater.
52. What is desalination?
Answer: Desalination is the process of removing dissolved salts from seawater to produce freshwater.
53. What is water treatment?
Answer: Water treatment is the process of removing contaminants or harmful substances from water.
54. Why should people protect oceans and freshwater?
Answer: Protecting water helps preserve ecosystems, natural resources and supplies needed by people and other organisms.
55. Why is water essential for life?
Answer: Water is essential for biological processes and is needed by plants, animals and humans to survive.
